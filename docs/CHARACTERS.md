# Characters: registry and complete-kit boundary

Canonical roster data: `src/js/56-roster.js`; playable starter configuration: `src/js/24b-data-solo.js`.
Names and stories come from [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md), [lore.md](design/lore.md) and the registry, not a new
naming pass. Wren Hollowmere is the archer, Tobin Reed the tank and Pip Cinderly the caster. Their current
resources are Aim, Grit and Cinders. Only these three have complete playable kits at this checkpoint.

The 32-hero goal does not make every registry entry playable. Each additional hero must ship a complete kit,
approved art, Hallowed looks, subclasses, unlock route and hero quest. Use existing unlock/Renown contracts
in `56c-unlocks.js` and ability sources in [MECHANICS.md](MECHANICS.md). Older companion circles and Bonds in
the lore are history, not permission to recreate the deleted party system.

When writing a brief, name the existing character key, role, established story, gameplay distinction, unlock
status and pack references. Label proposed changes separately. Art direction follows [ART_BIBLE.md](ART_BIBLE.md).

# C22 — Emberwaste dark-born enemy candidates

**Status:** exploratory candidate pool for owner review. This document does not lock the final enemy count, choose the released roster, define runtime data, or approve art. It proposes two strong normal-enemy concepts per area, then ranks the 28 concepts in the previous draft by how much of their combat identity is worth carrying forward. No old normal concept survives with its original origin unchanged. Ser Durand's established story remains the exception, and he is the region boss only. His fight mechanics and phase count remain proposals; the Pyre receives a separate boss candidate below. No boss is repeated or farmed.

## Direction and shared contract

Every candidate below is an active agent that formed from the Voice's darkness and Emberwaste's elemental conditions. None is a corrupted animal, escaped villager, ghost, animated household object, or captured lamp. Mineral and fire anatomy belongs to the being itself; it is not a material drop. This is a fantasy-origin rewrite, not a rename pass.

There is one enemy in a fight. No queued gauntlets, automatic starts, or offline combat are proposed. The player chooses each encounter. Enemy damage values are fixed zone-calibration percentages of the reference hero's maximum HP, not values that scale with the player's HP. The standard two-consecutive-opportunity limit applies to normal foes; an elder or boss may take at most three. Normal toughness aims at 3–5 uncritical Attack actions; named elite-base targets, if selected later, aim at 6–8. The final count and variant policy remain open.

Single-hit normal attacks stay in the 20–35% HP band. Multi-hit attacks state per-hit damage and their total; every actual contact has its own parry or dodge choice. Damage types are physical, holy, poison, fire, or frost, exactly one per hit. Physical armour is 10% light, 20% medium, or 30% heavy. Weakness is ×1.5; resistance is ×0.6. Status values and boss-charge handling follow [the shared C22 contract](enemies-c22-contract.md): Burn is 4% HP at the next two hero starts; Weaken cuts direct output 25% for the next hero opportunity; Blind affects the next direct action without hiding cues; Chill slows 10% per stack for two hero opportunities, maximum two stacks. A landed hit is required and each move applies its rider once unless stated.

All normal rewards remain Gold and area-grade Essence. Existing relic eligibility and routes stay unchanged; do not make every foe relic-eligible. Elders retain existing trophy eligibility and routes. Occasional boss uniques stay on already approved routes. Enemies award no ore, wood, fibre, hide, herb, gem, or other material. The approved gathering ladder remains separate from combat rewards. No candidate requires a new material or trophy item.

## Candidate pool — two per area

The first entry in each area is the stronger lead for review; the second is a reserve with a distinct silhouette and combat lesson. Both are proposals. Each card gives anatomy and origin, two moves, fixed combat targets, counterplay, rewards, and a seven-key normal art budget. Seven keys are idle, wind-up/contact, second-move tell/contact, hurt, and defeat; reuse contacts where possible.

### 1. Cinder Road

The road's black glass cracks open at dusk. The Voice's heat pours through the fissures and shapes agents around the moving ash. The area teaches a quick sequence and one heavy answer.

**Lead — Cinder Votary.** A tall, faceless ashborn with a narrow waist, six ember joints, and two hooked forearms that never touch the ground. It forms where road-cracks meet and follows a deliberate three-beat cadence; it is neither beast nor armour worn by a person. Speed `1.12`; toughness 4 actions; light physical armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Branding Rake:** slow-fast-fast, `3 × 8% physical = 24%`; each contact gets a separate defence. **Cinder Seal:** the Votary draws a bright line across its chest, holds, then sends one fire stroke for `1 × 27%`; on a landed hit, Burn applies once. **Answers:** Tobin can parry every Rake contact for one full-move counter or mix defences to stay safe; Wren can Mark during the long Seal tell; Pip should use frost rather than fire. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-7 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; the six ember joints make its idle silhouette readable.

**Reserve — Fissure Cantor.** A hovering knot of three black-glass masks joined by a vertical flame filament. The masks are faces the dark invents, not remnants of people; each turns to cue the next beat. Speed `1.04`; toughness 3 actions; no armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Threefold Spark:** slow-fast, `2 × 11% fire = 22%`. **Cauterizing Note:** one visible inhale and release, `1 × 23% holy`; a landed hit applies Weaken for one hero opportunity. **Answers:** react to the release, not the mask-turn; Wren can use a reliable marked shot, Tobin can parry the two distinct Sparks, and Pip can use Frost Shard. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-7 Essence; no material. **Art:** six keys; the three masks rotate within one body silhouette.

**Elder role — Mile-Crowned Adjudicator.** One immense ashborn judge with a crown of upright black-glass splinters and arms that divide into ember rails. Speed `1.05`; toughness 11 actions; medium armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Milebrand:** slow-fast-fast, `3 × 10% physical = 30%`. **Crown Sweep:** a bright rail forms across the chest before `1 × 29% physical`. **Fissure Sentence (charge):** planted tell, no damage; after one hero opportunity, release `3 × 14% fire = 42%`, interruptible before release by control or actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP. **Ashen Verdict:** held crown tilt then `1 × 24% fire`; on hit applies Weaken for one hero opportunity. **Answers:** parry all three Milebrand contacts for one counter, or interrupt Sentence and take its one recovery opportunity. **Trophy:** preserve the existing elder trophy route; no material reward. **Art:** provisional seven-key target; reuse shared arm contacts and a crown-light cue, then validate the poses in an art pass.

### 2. Emberlea Ruins

The dark rises from the empty road where the families escaped. These forms are newly made agents; none copies a resident, remembers a victim, or animates a door or other home object. The area teaches patient contact cues and short status pressure.

**Lead — Vacant Herald.** A long-limbed figure whose torso is a vertical split of grey ash; three floating shoulder plates orbit an empty center. It comes into being when the Voice speaks into the abandoned street. Speed `0.92`; toughness 4 actions; light armour; weak holy, resists fire. **Evacuation Step:** two measured contacts, `2 × 12% physical = 24%`. **Last Word:** the split torso opens with a clear held tell, then one `1 × 25% fire` contact; if it lands, apply Weaken for one hero opportunity. **Answers:** Tobin can parry both Step hits; Wren can use Mark during the held tell; Pip can choose holy or frost rather than fire. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-7 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; use three orbiting plates to communicate the wind-up.

**Reserve — Blackglass Magistrate.** A broad-shouldered dark-born duellist with a mirrored black face and forearms that unfold into flat, translucent blades. It is a single agent, not an empty suit or animated threshold. Speed `0.82`; toughness 5 actions; medium armour; weak holy, resists fire. **Crossing Cut:** slow-fast, `2 × 12% physical = 24%`. **Reeve's Measure:** held blade-level cue, then `1 × 31% physical`; on hit apply Weaken once. **Answers:** Sunder counters armour; the long Measure can be parried; Pip should not feed its fire resistance. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-7 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; blade unfolds on the hold, with no separate weapon prop.

**Elder role — Choir Without Names.** A column of six ashborn masks around a single black core. Each mask is a newly formed aspect; no face belongs to an Emberlea family. Speed `0.96`; toughness 12 actions; medium armour; weak holy, resists fire. **Staggered Petition:** three separate contacts, `3 × 10% physical = 30%`. **Ash Verdict:** a clear raised hand then `1 × 28% fire`; on hit applies Blind once. **The Unanswered Road (charge):** visible tell, no hit; after a hero opportunity release `3 × 13% physical = 39%`; interrupt by control or actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP. **Hollow Reply:** a wide arm turn then `2 × 12% physical = 24%`, slow-fast. **Answers:** defend each Petition contact; interrupt the charge or reserve a cleanse for Verdict. **Trophy:** existing elder eligibility and route remain. **Art:** provisional seven-key target; reuse shared arm contacts and mask-position changes, then validate the poses in an art pass.

### 3. The Ashfall

The sootfall is an elemental seam, not a nesting ground. Dark-born forms drift above the ground and use false descent cues before real contact.

**Lead — Veil Seraph.** A hovering, faceless figure with six long soot-streamers arranged like a broken halo; the streamers are its limbs, not wings on an animal. It condenses where ash obscures the Emberlea lamps. Speed `1.16`; toughness 3 actions; no armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Sable Descent:** a slow false drop, a clear pause, then one `1 × 22% fire` contact; on hit apply Burn once. **Streamer Fan:** quick-fast, `2 × 10% physical = 20%`. **Answers:** do not spend a defence on the false descent; Tobin can parry both Fan contacts; Pip uses frost. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-7 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; one full-body descent arc and six streamer angles.

**Reserve — Cinder Oracle.** A small, levitating dark-born with a crown of ash needles and a face that appears only at the contact cue. It is an agent, not an insect or spirit of a dead person. Speed `1.06`; toughness 4 actions; light armour; weak holy, resists fire. **Needle Choir:** three slow-fast-fast fire taps, `3 × 7% = 21%`. **False Face:** the face flashes, fades, then a true single `1 × 24% physical` strike; landed hit applies Blind for the next direct action. **Answers:** cue remains visible through Blind; Wren can wait for the true face; Pip can cleanse or attack with frost; Tobin parries each tap. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-7 Essence; no material. **Art:** six keys; face reveal is an overlay on the idle silhouette.

**Elder role — Sable Vesper.** A tall, four-armed dusk intelligence wrapped in a spiral of dense cinders; it is not a moth or a swarm. Speed `1.02`; toughness 11 actions; light armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Vesper Beat:** `3 × 10% fire = 30%`, slow-slow-fast. **Blackfall:** false descent followed by `2 × 12% fire = 24%`; last hit applies Burn once. **Night Veil:** the arms fold inward, then one `1 × 27% physical` sweep. **Nightfall Charge (charge):** no damage on tell; after one hero opportunity, `4 × 11% fire = 44%`; interrupt at actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP or with control. **Answers:** ignore the false descent, keep a defence for each real contact, then interrupt or answer the charge. **Trophy:** existing elder trophy route preserved. **Art:** provisional seven-key target; four arms share core contact frames, with cue overlays; validate in an art pass.

### 4. The Glass Flats

The old sand has fused into a dark mirror. These agents form around mineral planes but act with intent; none is an animal or a glass hazard attached to the arena.

**Lead — Prism Exarch.** A six-limbed, upright glass entity with a suspended obsidian core and angular limbs that reconfigure between attacks. It assembles from the darkness caught inside the Flats' fused sand. Speed `0.88`; toughness 5 actions; heavy armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Core Hammer:** a held, visible arm lock then `1 × 30% physical`. **Refracted March:** two separate cuts, `2 × 12% physical = 24%`; the second applies one Chill stack. **Answers:** Tobin can Sunder or parry the held strike; Wren can choose armour penetration; Pip's frost is favored. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-8 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; the suspended core stays centered as limbs move.

**Reserve — Mirror Apostle.** A narrow, faceless figure made from overlapping black mirror slivers. Its outline doubles briefly, but only its central body attacks; the reflection is never a second foe or fake reaction input. Speed `1.08`; toughness 4 actions; light armour; weak holy, resists fire. **Reflected Lunge:** a visible false image, pause, then one `1 × 24% physical` contact. **Shard Psalm:** slow-fast, `2 × 10% physical = 20%`; last hit applies Weaken for one hero opportunity. **Answers:** follow the central body; Wren may Mark during the pause, Tobin can Brace through the pair, and Pip can use holy or frost. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-8 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; mirrored outline is an overlay, not an extra pose.

**Elder role — Sovereign of the Flat.** A single dark-born monarch with a faceted chest cavity and a crown that folds into a cutting plane. Speed `0.96`; toughness 12 actions; heavy armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Glass Edict:** slow-fast, `2 × 16% physical = 32%`. **Mirror Reversal:** a false image followed by `2 × 12% physical = 24%`. **Crown Shear:** the crown unfolds, then `1 × 30% physical`. **Flatline Charge (charge):** no hit on tell; release `3 × 15% physical = 45%` after one hero opportunity; interrupt at actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP or with control. **Answers:** use Sunder or frost for armour, and defend each real contact. **Trophy:** preserve its existing elder eligibility and route. **Art:** provisional seven-key target; share the crown-plane contact frame and charge cue, then validate in an art pass.

### 5. The Kilns

The furnaces have gone cold, but darkness has learned to hold heat without fuel. The agents below are elemental wills, not kiln animals or animated tools.

**Lead — Cinder Titan.** A towering biped of black clinker plates with a bright molten seam running from chest to heel; each step leaves a momentary red crack that closes behind it. Speed `0.80`; toughness 5 actions; heavy armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Furnace Fist:** held shoulder turn then `1 × 33% physical`. **Slag Wake:** two slow fire contacts, `2 × 13% = 26%`; last hit applies Burn once. **Answers:** Tobin can Sunder and parry the long single hit; Wren's Pierce counters armour; Pip should use frost. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-8 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; seam brightens during the held tell.

**Reserve — Kilnbound Leaper.** A three-legged ash demon with a hollow furnace cavity and a long central limb used to vault; no animal head or natural anatomy. It forms from the first heatless updraft in an extinguished kiln. Speed `1.02`; toughness 4 actions; light armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Vault Impact:** an obvious false rise, then one `1 × 22% physical` landing. **Cinder Spit:** delayed-fast, `2 × 10% fire = 20%`; last hit adds one Burn. **Answers:** wait for the real landing cue; Wren can Pinned, Tobin can Brace across the pair, Pip uses frost. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-8 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; use a clear crouch, vault, and landing silhouette.

**Elder role — Furnace Oracle.** A robed, dark-born giant whose body is a column of rolling slag and whose hands are two suspended white-hot rings. Speed `0.86`; toughness 13 actions; heavy armour; weak frost, resists fire. **White Ring:** delayed `1 × 34% physical`. **Kiln Pulse:** slow-fast, `2 × 15% fire = 30%`; last hit Burn. **Seal the Heat (charge):** no damage on tell; release `4 × 12% fire = 48%` after a hero opportunity; interrupt at actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP or by control. **Ringfall:** the rings rise apart, then close for `2 × 14% physical = 28%`. **Answers:** target the cold seam with frost, Sunder the shell, or spend a control move during the held charge. **Trophy:** preserve current elder trophy eligibility and route. **Art:** provisional seven-key target; ring rotation supplies cues while contact frames are shared; validate in an art pass.

### 6. Wyrmscale Ridge

The Ashen Wyrm remains a separate world-raid foe. Emberwaste candidates here are lesser dark-born drakes and elemental intelligences: they are not wildlife changed by the Wyrm or members of its brood.

**Lead — Obsidian Drake.** A small but complete dark-born drake with a split-glass crest, four hovering scale-plates, and a flame channel running through its throat. It emerged when Wyrm heat crossed a darkness seam, not from an egg or parent. Speed `1.12`; toughness 3 actions; light armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Scale Chime:** slow-fast, `2 × 11% physical = 22%`. **Cinder Exhale:** held throat glow then one `1 × 24% fire`; a landed hit applies Burn once. **Answers:** Pip should use frost; Tobin can parry both Chime contacts; Wren can Mark during the held Exhale. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-9 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; floating plates orbit one drake body.

**Reserve — Ashen Chimera.** A wholly fantastic three-faced elemental with one shared torso, six arms, and a tail of unbound flame. The faces are facets of one dark-born mind, not animal heads grafted together. Speed `0.94`; toughness 5 actions; medium armour; weak holy, resists fire. **Threefold Reach:** three separate physical contacts, `3 × 9% = 27%`. **Flame Axis:** a visible turn then one `1 × 28% fire` hit; last landed contact applies Chill for two hero opportunities. **Answers:** parry every Reach hit for one counter or mix defence choices; Pip should use holy/frost instead of fire; Wren can Mark through the slow turn. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-9 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; three faces share one hurt and contact pose.

**Elder role — Nightfire Arcanist.** A tall, wingless draconic intelligence made from plates of dark basalt, with a humanoid stance and a ring of floating embers. It is not the Ashen Wyrm. Speed `1.00`; toughness 14 actions; medium armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Basalt Sever:** slow-fast, `2 × 16% physical = 32%`. **Nightfire Breath:** held cue then `2 × 13% fire = 26%`; last hit adds Burn once. **Ridgebreak (charge):** tell, then `4 × 12% fire = 48%` after one hero opportunity; interrupt at actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP or with control. **Cinder Axis:** a bright ring turns edge-on, then `1 × 29% fire`. **Answers:** separate it from the raid boss in name and scale; use frost or control, and defend every Breath contact. **Trophy:** existing elder trophy route stays intact. **Art:** provisional seven-key target; share arm and breath contacts while the ring of embers cues the move; validate in an art pass.

### 7. The Pyre

The stolen lights remain part of Ser Durand's established story. Ordinary Pyre foes are separate dark-born agents drawn to the tower; they are not the lights themselves, lamps, sconces, weapons left in the room, or captives.

**Lead — Gloam Duelist.** A tall, dark-born swordsman with an empty chest crossed by a single thread of reflected lamp-light and a mantle that divides into two shadow arms. The thread is not a soul or a harvestable object. Speed `1.10`; toughness 4 actions; light armour; weak frost, resists fire. **Oathless Cut:** slow-fast, `2 × 11% physical = 22%`. **Blackbrand:** a clear sword-draw hold, then `1 × 25% fire`; landed hit applies Blind to the next direct action. **Answers:** the contact cue remains visible; Tobin can parry both Cut hits; Pip should open with frost; Wren can Mark during the hold. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-9 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; no lamp or shield prop.

**Reserve — Pyre Wraith-Captain.** A broad, faceless figure with a crown of floating ember-script and a lower body that dissolves into a single dark ribbon. It condenses near stolen-light pressure, but is not a Kept Light. Speed `0.86`; toughness 5 actions; medium armour; weak holy, resists fire. **Ribbon Sweep:** delayed-fast, `2 × 13% physical = 26%`. **Crownflare:** held crown turn then one `1 × 30% fire` contact; landed hit applies Weaken for one hero opportunity. **Answers:** Sunder the armour, use holy/frost, and defend the held contact rather than the crown's earlier turn. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-9 Essence; no material. **Art:** seven keys; ribbon is part of one body, not an added actor.

**Story exception — Ser Durand, the Pyre Knight.** Preserve the established Caedmon/Durand story, his yes/no bargain, and the stolen lights returning home. Durand is the region boss only, not the Pyre area boss or elder. His mechanics and phase count remain undecided; do not treat the prior four-move kit or three-phase script as approved. His encounter is unique and cannot be repeated or farmed. Do not replace the established story with a new origin or make a stolen light a reward. The Pyre has its own separate area-boss candidate below, distinct from Durand; whether it also serves as the area elder remains pending.

**Proposed Pyre area boss — The Starless Marshal.** A separate, dark-born agent formed where the Pyre's stolen-light pressure strikes cooled obsidian. It is a floating, broad-shouldered figure with a vertical black-sun aperture in its chest and four jointed arms; it is not a lamp, knight, or captive. It does not repeat Ser Durand. Speed `1.04`; toughness 15 actions; medium armour; weak frost, resists fire, neutral physical/holy/poison. **Meridian Stroke:** two slow-fast physical hits at 14% each (28% total). **Black Noon (charge):** the chest aperture opens in a no-damage tell; after one hero opportunity it releases four fire hits at 12% each (48% total). Interrupt before release with control or actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP; Ward damage does not count, DoT HP damage does. **Starless Reach:** held arm extension then one `1 x 30% physical` hit; on hit applies Weaken for one hero opportunity. **Crown of Ash:** clear arm-cross tell then two fire hits at 12% each (24% total); last hit applies Burn once. **Answers:** interrupt Black Noon or defend four individually cued hits; frost counters its resistance profile, while Wren's Mark, Tobin's armour tools and Pip's Frost Shard each have an answer. **Rewards:** Gold + grade-9 Essence; preserve current boss relic eligibility and allow a unique only on an existing approved route. One-time encounter only; no repeat farm and no material drops. **Trophy:** if this role is approved as an elder, keep the existing elder trophy route. **Art:** provisional seven-key target; share arm contacts and use the aperture as a cue overlay; validate in an art pass. This distinct Pyre candidate is proposed without fixing the region's final boss count, phase structure, or area pass order.

## Standalone elder roles and reward treatment

The six elder concepts above are candidate replacements for the earlier animal/object-based elder concepts; their final selection is open. The Starless Marshal is one separate Pyre elder/boss candidate. Keep the shared elder charge rule: the charge spends an opportunity without damage, gives at least one hero response, and releases on a later eligible opportunity. Interrupt with control or actual HP damage equal to `6%` of boss maximum HP; Ward damage does not count and DoT HP damage does. An interrupted charge consumes the move and grants one recovery, never a second control skip. Each elder concept sketches three regular moves and one charged move; selected kits may be trimmed to three or four total moves after review. Elders retain trophy eligibility on existing approved routes, receive Gold and area-grade Essence, and never award gathering materials.

All six concepts target 11–14 ordinary, uncritical Attack actions and use 7 art keys. Their move and damage values are calibration proposals. Elder trophies are intentionally not renamed or newly assigned here.

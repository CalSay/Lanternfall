# C22: Sunken Coast enemy pool and one-on-one encounters

Status: design proposal for owner and Claude review, 1 October 2026. No runtime or art is changed here. Read with [C22 brief](enemies-c22.md), [Coast plan](region-2.md), [hero abilities](hero-abilities.md) §2a, [Coast lore](lore.md) §4.3 and the approved [resource ladder](../../art/resources/regional-audit/complete-ladder.json). The owner's newer **active-only combat** decision overrides the old Coast plan's Auto, idle, formation, timer and party-survival advice. These are relative tuning targets, not measured balance.

The seven areas repeat across zones 36–69; zone 70 is Saltreach Light. An area fight draws three to five sequential foes from that area's four-normal pool, never four enemies on stage. A named tougher variant can replace one slot (rarity to tune, at most one per gauntlet. Variants inherit every unlisted parent field and move; their stated differences are the complete modifier, not a second actor). The variant is already the **6–8-action elite base**; an elite trait must not double that HP again. The area elder appears as the final single opponent of an elder encounter, not alongside its summons. This preserves the coast's ecology while giving each solo fight a distinct timing lesson. Silas is a separate region-boss attempt.

## Card notation and safety envelope

Every speed is relative to a zone-ready hero. At 0.7–1.0×, no expected consecutive foe actions; at 1.15×, roughly one double after eight hero opportunities; at 1.3×, roughly one after three to four; at 1.5×, roughly one after two. These are **expectations for a proposed gauge**, not a promise of an exact cadence; normal foes never exceed two consecutive actions and bosses never exceed three. “HP 4” means about four uncritical ordinary Attack actions to kill with level-appropriate gear, neutral damage and typical zone armour already reflected. Normal HP is 3–5, named variant 6–8, elder 10–15. Armour 0/1/2 means none/light/heavy physical mitigation; weakness and resistance use only physical, holy, poison, fire or frost, at most one weakness and two resistances, with no immunity.

Move notation gives **damage per hit as percent of a zone-ready reference hero's maximum HP** before armour, Ward or successful defence, followed by damage type, timing, status and an exact visible/audio tell. Step 3 converts these percentages to fixed zone attack values; actual damage never scales upward merely because a player buys more maximum HP. Each hit permits its own parry-or-dodge choice. A parried hit reduces every ability cooldown by one; only an all-parried move earns the counter. Normal missed-move totals are roughly 20–35% HP; a split combo's total, not every hit, uses that budget. “Failure budget 3” means three comparable full moves may be missed from full HP; the next is expected to kill, before DoT or healing. Status is applied only by the named hit that lands, once per move. Bleed 1 means 1% reference HP at the start of each of the next two hero opportunities; Venom 1 means 1% at each of the next three; Burn 1 means 2% at each of the next two. Reapplication keeps the stronger tick and refreshes duration rather than stacking hidden damage. Chill 1 slows Speed 10% for two hero opportunities (two-stack cap); Weaken 1 cuts direct output 25% for the next hero opportunity; Blind 1 gives the next direct hero action a 30% miss chance but never hides a cue or changes a parry/dodge choice. These turn durations and magnitudes are proposals to align the cards; C19/step 3 must settle the actual hero-status contract. A visible icon plus pose carries each tell; colour alone never does. No attack is parry-only or dodge-only.

Every base foe uses a seven-key planning rig: idle, main wind-up and contact, alternate wind-up and contact, hurt, defeated. One-move foes can use five. A variant shares the parent's anatomical rig, but its changed contact, prop or FX must be authored and reviewed; never recolour an incompatible action. Elders reuse family keys where physically credible, with seven or fewer body keys and distinct authored hit/charge effects. Encounter rewards are Gold and Essence, with only existing eligible relic rolls; elder bosses may occasionally award their existing unique. Foes do not award wood, fibre, herbs, gems, ore, hide or other gathered materials. The approved Coast grade ladder remains a separate gathering and Hunting concern; hide grades 4–6 come from Riptide Shark, Tide Kelpie and Brine Basilisk, not combat rewards. Existing nonmaterial story unlocks remain intact.

### Audit of the seven designed Coast foes and elders

| Designed foe / elder | Decision | One-on-one reason |
|---|---|---|
| Shinglecrab / Elder Shinglecrab | Keep / rework | Shell timing and exposed seams make a good single target. Replace seconds-long near-immunity and repeated passive healing with a brief, interruptible shell charge and readable armour windows. |
| Stormgull / Elder Stormgull | Keep / rework | The dive and squall read well alone. Retire targeting “the party's biggest shield” and mass shield stripping; give one player-visible wing rhythm and parryable talons. |
| Drowned Deckhand / the Bosun | Keep / rework | The lost sailor and ship bell are core Coast lore. Retire Front/Mid dragging and simultaneous crew; bell can schedule one deckhand later in the gauntlet and make an interruptible solo sound-combo now. |
| Kelp Strangler / Elder Kelp Strangler | Keep / rework | The boat-long eel's coil is a strong solo silhouette. Retire “bind two party members”; a landed coil weakens or chills this one hero, with a tell and a break opportunity. |
| Lanternjelly / Elder Lanternjelly | Keep / rework | Stolen light is compelling, but “chain to adjacent party members” and three simultaneous splits have no solo target. Make pulses sequential hits and any split a later gauntlet entry, never three stage actors. |
| Brine Witch / Elder Brine Witch | Keep / rework | Her healing/hex ritual is readable as a charge. Replace “top damage dealer/healer” selection with a marked single hero and an interruptible self-heal. |
| Coral Warden / Elder Coral Warden | Keep / rework | Chapel guardian and reef wall fit the final area. Retire physical immunity and unrestricted reflect; use a one-opportunity guard with a weak seam and a bounded counter cue. |

The Coast rows already exist as design data in 21x-data-types.js and 21g-data-bosses.js but are not the live Region 2 enemy pool. Their old pack sizes, rows, type multipliers and seconds-based mechanics are **audit inputs**, not numbers to carry into turn combat. All seven identities survive; their party-only behaviours do not.

The source type table still treats Kelp Strangler as “drowned” even though Coast lore calls it an eel; this proposal classifies its combat weakness as beast-like poison. It also removes inherited fire resistance from drowned people and Lanternjelly so Pip's direct fire is viable (high tide still halves Burn), while preserving holy weakness on the drowned. Shells use physical armour rather than a second physical resistance. These are deliberate future type-data changes, not claims that the current FOE_TYPE rows already behave this way. The old Stormgull feather-as-Hide combat drop is retired; Stormgrass Fibre belongs to gathering.
## Grey Shingle — first lesson: shells and the tide line

The exposed stones leave room for side-stepping, not a permanent armour wall. Its four normal foes are Shinglecrab, Tide Skipper, Pebble Limpet and Drowned Netter. At low tide, shell armour is heavy and Burn ticks at 1.25×; at high tide, shell armour drops one tier and Burn ticks at 0.5×. This preserves the Coast's tide contrast without the old Front-column/formation rules. Turning tide changes the waterline only; no unauthored stage surge or hidden hit is added. All four have a dry-rock contact point for their art.

- **Shinglecrab.** A shore crab whose shell thickened under the dark. Speed 0.75× (no double); HP 5; armour 2 while closed, 1 while claws spread; weak poison, resist none. **Claw Tap:** 1 hit, 26% physical, slow; left claw rises above its eyestalks and clicks once before contact. **Rake:** 2 hits, 12% + 14% physical (26 total), slow–fast; both claws open in sequence, final landed hit adds Bleed 1. Tests whether the player sees an exposed seam rather than wasting physical damage into shell. Failure budget 3 (Bleed can make it 2). Wren's Mark/Barbed Arrow and Pip's fire-neutral spell punish the opening; Tobin's Brace handles Rake. Reward: Gold and Essence; existing eligible relic rolls unchanged. Art: seven keys, with open and closed shell overlays.
- **Breaker Crab** (Shinglecrab variant). Speed 0.9× (no double); HP 8; armour 2 closed/1 open; weak poison, resist none. Inherits Claw Tap at 30% and Rake at 15% + 16% (Bleed 1); **Cracked Shell** adds 3 hits of 10% + 11% + 12% physical (33 total), slow–slow–fast, with a raised split shell and three audible cracks. Total missed moves 30–33%; failure budget 2 once Bleed lands. Tests multi-hit discipline; Wren can Mark the seam, Tobin can parry the three cracks for cooldowns, Pip attacks while open. Reward: Gold and Essence; existing eligible relic rolls unchanged. Art: parent seven keys plus a unique split-shell contact overlay.
- **Tide Skipper.** A small fish darkened while stranded in tide pools, leaping between wet stones. Speed 1.35× (about one double per three to four hero opportunities); HP 3; armour 0; weak poison, resist none. **Fin Flick:** 1 hit, 22% physical, quick; its tail snaps vertical and its reflection breaks. **Skip Bite:** 2 hits, 11% + 13% physical (24 total), quick–delayed; it lands on one stone, pauses a beat, then bites from the next. Tests tracking a fast delayed second hit. Failure budget 4 before attrition; Wren's Pinning Shot curbs its rhythm, Tobin's shield/parry earns two refunds, Pip's Spark ends the short fight. Reward: Gold and Essence; existing eligible relic rolls unchanged. Art: seven keys, two distinct stone-contact frames.
- **Razorfin Skipper** (Tide Skipper variant). Speed 1.5× (about one double per two hero opportunities); HP 6; armour 0; weak poison, resist none. Fin Flick is 27%; Skip Bite is 14% + 15%. **Backwash** adds 3 hits of 10% + 11% + 12% frost (33 total), fast–pause–fast; it cuts a V-shaped wake before each contact and the final landed hit adds Chill 1. Failure budget 2 with Chill. Tests faster turn frequency; Wren's Mark/Deadeye can finish it, Tobin's Brace widens one defence, Pip's fire stays neutral. Reward: Gold and Essence; existing eligible relic rolls unchanged. Art: parent seven keys plus an authored V-wake effect.
- **Pebble Limpet.** A limpet fused to an old sea-wall stone. Speed 0.7× (no double); HP 5; armour 2 at low tide/1 at high tide; weak frost, resist poison. **Seal Slam:** 1 hit, 31% physical, long delay; it lifts its entire shell and the underside turns pale before dropping. **Grit Spray:** 2 hits, 13% + 14% physical (27 total), even; two visible stone chips leave the rim, final landed chip adds Blind 1. Tests a slow but unforgiving tell. Failure budget 3, Blind may reduce it; Wren's Mark softens the shell, Tobin can parry the single slam, Pip's Frost Shard hits the weakness. Reward: Gold and Essence; existing eligible relic rolls unchanged. Art: seven keys, including the lifted underside.
- **Iron Limpet** (Pebble Limpet variant). Speed 0.85× (no double); HP 8; armour 2 at low tide/1 at high tide; weak frost, resist poison. Seal Slam is 34%; Grit Spray is 15% + 16% with Blind on the last. **Shingle Burst:** 3 hits, 10% + 11% + 13% physical (34 total), slow–fast–fast; shell seams glow before three stone chips leave one at a time. Failure budget 2; tests whether the player can defend each projectile rather than the first only. Wren uses Barbed Arrow through armour, Tobin can guard the last hit, Pip's Frost Shard is favored. Reward: Gold and Essence; existing eligible relic rolls unchanged. Art: parent seven keys plus three distinct projectile cues.
- **Drowned Netter.** A fisher who kept casting a net after the boats stopped returning. Speed 1.0× (no double); HP 4; armour 0; weak holy, resist frost. **Lead Knot:** 1 hit, 25% physical, delayed; the weighted knot hangs back, then flicks forward. **Net Cast:** 2 hits, 11% + 14% physical (25 total), slow–fast; the arm circles once, and the second landed hit adds Weaken for one hero opportunity. No immobilisation. Tests the fake pause at arm's length. Failure budget 3; Wren uses Power Shot or Mark; Tobin's Brace answers the second hit; Pip's Fireball is neutral. Reward: Gold and Essence; existing eligible relic rolls unchanged. Art: seven keys with two net states.
- **Reef Netter** (Drowned Netter variant). Speed 1.15× (rare double, about one per eight hero opportunities); HP 7; armour 1 from coral knots; weak holy, resist frost. Lead Knot is 29%; Net Cast is 14% + 16%, Weaken on the last. **Weighted Sweep:** 2 hits, 17% + 18% physical (35 total), slow–fast; the reef stones sewn into the net appear before the arc starts. Suggested four-foe gauntlet: Tide Skipper → Shinglecrab → Drowned Netter → Pebble Limpet. A variant can replace its parent. The elder follows only in an elder encounter, after a player-controlled stop/continue boundary; HP follows the agreed healing rule, without an undocumented full heal.

## Gullcliffs — lesson: air, delayed landings and wind

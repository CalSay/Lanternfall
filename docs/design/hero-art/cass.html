Cass Penhallow

Selected existing hero

Weathered adult female coastal hunter, brisk practical expression, wind-tangled dark hair tied back. Waxed short sea coat in faded petrol blue, rope sash, salt-worn boots, two-handed hooked harpoon held close and planted. Coral-rust details, sea-worn buckles. No thrown harpoon, loose nets or pirate costume.

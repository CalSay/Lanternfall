Inga Fallow

Selected existing hero

Adult woman earthy star surveyor, obsessive sceptical face, practical dark braid. Angled surveying hood, slate-brown field coat, shard-capped auger staff held grounded, measuring chain and brass calipers. Earth ochre with small cold star glint. Ground survey geometry, no celestial queen crown or Oriel robe.

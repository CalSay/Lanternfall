Oriel Vess

Selected existing hero

Adult female sky reader, upright and dryly scrutinising, dark blue-black hair in a practical knot. Midnight-blue and cream robe, tall staff with tiny warm star fragment enclosed in brass, brass folding sky-chart frame. Precise celestial geometry, restrained pale-gold glow. No giant moon crown or fire mage costume.

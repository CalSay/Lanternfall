Ragna Vik

Selected existing hero

Older adult female winter forager with sardonic face and grey braids. Shaggy herb mantle over moss and plum wool, hooked held staff, closed kettle secured to belt, winter boots. Lichen-grey, muted violet, herb green. Rugged botanical craft, no enormous hat, Pip fire staff or loose throwable kettle.

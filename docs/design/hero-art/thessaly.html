Thessaly Gloam

Selected existing hero

Tall adult woman marsh seer, patient evasive face, dark damp-looking braided hair. Marsh-grey and muted teal reed-pattern robes, shallow sealed jar at belt, crooked reed-topped staff. Wading boots, layered reed shawl, subtle pale marsh-light accent. No combat stilts, giant witch hat or fire staff.

# C22 fantasy exploration — the Gloamvale

**Exploration candidates only; no roster size or selection is approved.** The owner has replaced the old origin constraint: combat enemies are agents born or newly emerged with the darkness, not corrupted wildlife, echoes of dead animals, remnants of villagers or possessed household objects. This direct instruction supersedes the contrary general lore rule. Corrupted existing wildlife belongs to Hunting, which is not being converted into combat here.

This file explores twelve directions, two per area, while the owner and Claude discuss count. It does not replace the original `enemies-c22-gloamvale.md`, approve twelve encounters, or require every idea to survive. Whitehush and the Voice retain their established story exceptions; this document makes no final decision about the Voice's phases.

## Ground rules

The Gloamvale remains an outdoor grey-violet valley under a closed sky, distinct from the Deepwell. Dead woodland, unrotting crop and abandoned buildings remain **environment**, not combat bodies. New predatory forms can have emerged from the darkness here without inventing a restored ordinary ecology, local villagers to fight, or Hunting animals that crossed into the roster. Bodies read as ink-dark, bone-grey or dim violet; the hero's lamp remains the warm focal point.

One hero fights **one enemy per fight**. There are no queues, gauntlets, simultaneous adds or automatic next fights. All hero actions require player selection. Absence/pausing cannot produce combat progress or rewards. Every actual hit accepts parry or dodge; each parry refunds one from every active cooldown, and only an all-parry completed move produces one counter. Artist-made cues identify each contact; decorative shadow, feints and overlapping limbs do no damage. Speed changes turn frequency, never reaction-window width.

Figures are comparison proposals, not approved tuning: toughness 3–5 neutral uncritical ordinary Attack actions; Speed 1.0 alternates, faster candidates can sometimes double, slower candidates sometimes let the hero double, normal cap two. Percentage damage is calibrated into fixed zone stats against reference HP; it never scales up with the player's purchased HP. Armour is physical only. Weakness/resistance use phys, holy, poison, fire, frost; all unlisted types are neutral. Timings are rhythm descriptions pending animation/accessibility testing.

A listed Bleed or Venom deals 2% reference HP on each of two subsequent hero starts, strongest refresh with no stacking/ramp. Chill is one 10% reference Speed stack for two subsequent hero opportunities, maximum two stacks under the shared contract; no hero Freeze or stolen action. Weaken reduces direct output 25% for one hero opportunity. Status lands only on its specifically marked undefended HP-damaging contact, once per move; a fully absorbed Ward carrier does not apply it in this regional proposal. No listed rider means no status.

Rewards remain **gold and Essence**, with relics only under existing eligibility and boss uniques only through approved occasional tables. Elders retain **Trophies**, existing first-clear flags and progression unlocks. Kills yield no materials, Sigils or disguised landscape caches. No extra relic roll, new currency, art asset or runtime implementation is authorized by these sketches.

## The Last Descent — exploration candidates

### Riftmaw

**Concept and anatomy:** a low, five-legged hunter crawled out of the darkness beneath the road when the sky closed. Its mouth is two complete jaw rings mounted one behind the other; the front ring can turn sideways independently. A thick central rear leg acts as a brace, making its rising strike an unmistakable silhouette. It was never a dog, wolf or person.

**Profile:** Speed 1.25, occasional doubles, cap 2; toughness 3; armour 0%; weak fire, no resistance.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |
|---|---|---|
| First Mouth | 23% phys, one compact hit | Front jaw ring opens sideways, locks, then turns toward the hero for contact. No invisible contact from the turning head. |
| Mouth Behind | 12%+12%=24% phys, quick then delayed | Front ring bites; the larger rear ring stays fully open before its separate second closure. No rider. |

**Test/answers:** the first closed mouth does not end the move. Wren's Pinned eases both reactions; Tobin can earn Grit from either parry; Pip exploits fire weakness or slows the timeline with Frost Shard. **Buildability:** seven keys—five-leg crouch, front-ring open/bite, double-ring open/closure, hurt, jaw rings collapsing around the dark core. Jaw layers make the two contacts independent without adding a second actor. **Rewards:** shared normal rules.

### Dusk Herald

**Concept and anatomy:** a broad-shouldered emissary emerged on the pass before the first light could enter the valley. Four jointed arms surround a headless neck aperture; each elbow is a closed bony loop. Its central chest slit opens vertically into the body. No cloak, toll purse, weapon or remembered human face supplies its identity.

**Profile:** Speed 1.0, alternating; toughness 4; armour 5%; weak fire, no resistance.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |
|---|---|---|
| Ringed Palm | 22% phys, one deliberate hit | One elbow loop opens into a hooked forearm, then the broad hand turns outward before striking. |
| Breath of Dusk | 24% poison, one held release | The chest slit widens and remains still, then compresses around one visible grey-violet ribbon. Venom 2% for two hero starts on HP damage,28% maximum including ticks. |

**Test/answers:** the chest opening is preparation, not an unavoidable poison field. Wren can Mark and attack; Tobin uses Ward to absorb the carrier or Bash for control; Pip's fire exploits weakness. **Buildability:** seven keys—closed-aperture idle, loop-open/palm strike, chest-open/ribbon release, hurt, inward body collapse. One authored ribbon contact, no lingering cloud. **Rewards:** shared normal rules.

**Profile:** Speed 1.2, occasional doubles; toughness 4; armour 5%; weak holy, no resistance.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |

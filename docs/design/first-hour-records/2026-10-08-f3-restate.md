# Ruling: F3, the shape of the first hour (f3-restate), 2026-10-08

Opus high judge, independent of the W5 report's author. Repo: integration branch at a4cd1a43. Cal can veto (phrase at the end).
Question: adopt, amend or reject the F3 text proposed in W5 section 5 (`autopilot/reports/why/W5-2026-10-08.md` in the project
files), against W5 and the 2026-10-06 ruling (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, Early game). A red team argued against it first (below).

## Ruling

**Amend.** Drop the 5-minute floor and keep the 8-minute stall alarm, counted only on moments of real progress. Name the three
peaks, with upper time bounds only. Say that F3 covers zones 1 to 10 and is never a reason by itself to keep, add or fold a card.
Leave the fights clock, the person-minute ratio and any lower time bounds to the 2026-11-02 review, when real players' times exist.

The ruled text (DECISIONS.md and `docs/design/first-hour.md` quote it):

> **F3, the shape of the first hour** (zones 1 to 10; replaces the 2026-10-06 wording).
> (a) **No dead stretch.** On the casual walk, never more than 8 minutes without a progress moment, up to the zone 10 Champion
> (or minute 60, if that comes first). A progress moment is a zone's first clear, a new ability, a Star, a hero joining or a
> unique. Level cards, hero lines, looks and crafts do not count: a grind or a wall makes those on its own.
> (b) **Three peaks, rising.** The first boss win and its cache (F2), the zone 5 Champion with the first companion, and the
> zone 10 Champion closing the chapter. Each has its own big card that says what it gave. A casual person reaches the zone 5
> Champion by minute 30 and the zone 10 Champion by minute 60. Later is a miss; earlier is never a miss and never a reason to
> slow the game.
> (c) **Every big card says what it gave.** A tester can name what each big card gave them.
> F3 sets nothing past zone 10 (the 2026-11-02 review does). "5 to 10" in older cards means zones, never minutes. F3 alone is
> never a reason to keep, add or fold a card.

## Why

- **The floor has no source; the cap does.** W5's three research lanes found no designer or study giving a big-moment interval
  (W5 section 3, point 1). The 8-minute cap matches Booth ("Long periods of inactivity are boring") and Hopson on the low after a
  reward. Every gap the old F3 flagged was a stall with its own fix in the game, not a missing card: the zone 2 grind and a 25 s
  tip freeze (desk notes 3:37 to 11:45), the zone 9 to 10 death loop (leg2 notes 37 to 60, fixed by #253), and the bot's
  Woodcutting detour (walk 10:31 to 22:16).
- **The floor is already used to defend cards.** DECISIONS "Moment cap" (2026-10-07) kept the zone 1 to 3 look caches because
  they "are F3's 'big every 5 minutes'". `z13-casual-margin` (line 21) and `walk-bot-gear` (line 10) read "5 to 10" as minutes,
  and the first applied it to zones 11 to 15, where F3 sets nothing.
- **W5's "medium or big moment" would let a stall pass (red team 1, checked).** In `src/js/75-moments-ui.js`, a hero line
  ("bark") is a medium moment on every non-region boss clear (:242) and on the first loss at each new zone (:278); a level card
  fires at level 2 and every fifth level (:264), and the desk player got the level 15 card mid-grind (leg2 note 34). A wall
  makes its own medium moments, so (a) counts progress only. The walk already counts zone clears, uniques and Stars as big
  (`tools/walk.mjs:676-679`), so (a) is close to what it scores today, minus the floor.
- **The person clock is not measured yet (red team 3, checked).** Both "person" runs were model players or driver runs (the desk
  player is an Opus persona on a stepped clock; cold player B reloaded each call). The ratio runs 2.0 to 5.7 by zone, and the
  beat map uses 2.4. So no conversion goes into the rule now, and the windows keep only their upper ends, which the evidence
  does support: the desk run cleared zone 5 at 23:03 and zone 10 at 45:14 with no rally gates; cold player B reached zone 10
  at about 59. A lower bound would fail a faster game for being faster, and the map's est puts zone 5 at 17:45.
- **Waiting for 2 Nov (red team 9) costs more than amending now.** The floor keeps defending cards every day it stands, and
  `rally-gates-live` needs a zone 10 target now. The parts that need human data wait for 2 Nov.

## Red team (Opus, read only), and what the ruling did with each point

1. Medium moments let a stall excuse itself. **Taken:** (a) counts progress moments only.
2. Counting fights misses gathering stalls. **Taken:** (a) stays in game time; the fights clock is dropped from the rule.
3. The person-minute is a modelled clock. **Taken:** no conversion in the rule until a human run sets one.
4. The windows disagree with the map's est and punish a faster game. **Taken:** upper bounds only.
5. The Monday hold loses its baseline. **Taken:** see "Until the walk changes" below.
6. (b) and (c) cannot be checked by the walk. **Taken:** "bigger" is defined as its own big card that says what it gave; (b)'s
   times and (c) are tester and player checks, not exact checks.
7. Dropping F3 as a reason pulls support from the moment-cap rulings. **Partly:** the 2026-10-08 shape rule (one big card per
   zone clear) stands on its own reasons and is out of this card's scope. No new fold rule is added (process freeze).
8. The text dropped "or minute 60". **Taken:** restored.
9. Waiting is cheaper. **Rejected,** for the reason above.

What survived the red team: the 8-minute stall alarm, dropping the floor, "zones 1 to 10 only", (c)'s aim and a zone 10 target
inside the hour.

## Until the walk changes

The walk keeps scoring F3 as it does today (`tools/walk.mjs:853`); this card never touches it. Until its scoring follows (a),
the Sunday go or hold reads F3 by the longest gap up to the zone 10 clear, which the walk already prints, and the 5:00 floor
is report-only. The M0 count of two Monday builds starts again for F3 the week the new scoring lands. Changing the walk is a
Foreman card (`walk-f3-in-fights`, re-scoped).

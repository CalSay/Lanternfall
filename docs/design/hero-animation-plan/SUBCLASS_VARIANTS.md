# Subclass and Star variants: reuse the same motion pack

The six subclass paths and 72 revised Stars in `docs/design/hero-abilities-34.json` are proposals. Their modifiers do not add a fourth action, an automatic defence, an autonomous attack or a new held weapon. This supplements the complete base-card manifest; it does not add duplicate body drawings to its counts.

| Path | Existing motion and effect reuse | Release and state rule |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior → Warden | Ordinary manually earned defence and chosen Attack; Brace protection and Sunder contact sequences. Reuse the hero’s existing guard/ward application and finite held-charge presentation. | Shelter appears only after the qualifying whole defended move; its later Attack rider resolves within that Attack. Brace Ward arrives after defence; Sunder’s selected Weaken replaces the traded portion of damage. No immediate counter or automatic shield block. |
| Warrior → Reaver | Existing Attack/Cleave/Heavy Strike body and contact layers; existing finite Bleed and Guard effects. | Edge of Battle primes a later Attack, not a new strike. Cleave’s consumed Bleed contracts before one derived payout. Heavy Strike’s chosen Guard replaces its Opening damage rider. |
| Ranger → Venomstalker | Each actual hero’s bow or held-blade/spear motion; pooled Venom application/consumption and existing Mark layers. | Venom is one authored application under its stack/tick budget. Barbed Strike’s Venom replaces Bleed; Quarry Mark’s trade resolves within one action. No animated snake actor, extra arrow or thrown spear. |
| Ranger → Pathfinder | Manual defence → ordinary chosen Attack; Pinning Strike’s existing contact; Power Strike’s ordinary motion with its lighter Footwork presentation. | Route is earned after complete defence and spent on the later Attack’s Pin. Footwork uses the same return-to-home envelope for melee or planted bow release for archers. Speed is the printed benefit; there is no automatic evasion or extra turn. |
| Mage → Spellweaver | Existing spell and basic Attack body poses, including Inga’s physical staff contact. Frost Shard reuses its frost charge/flight/impact and an existing consumed-Burn cue. Nova uses the caster’s already authored effect pack for the selected previous spell type. | One follow-up token per chosen action. A consumed Burn tick cannot tick again. Nova changes only the selected damage-type presentation, not the previous spell’s status payload. Reuse matching existing type assets; do not recolour a frozen pack in code or invent a second elemental hit. |
| Mage → Lightkeeper | Existing Ward end/break and held-charge cues; ordinary next Attack/spell, Arcane Ward and Spark body/effect sequences. | Warmth is earned on the actual natural Ward end/break and waits for the chosen payoff. Arcane Ward’s smaller status-block mode changes the finite protection confirmation, not its pose. Spark’s small Ward arrives after contact; it is not reflected damage. |

Subclass tokens must remain source-labelled in the UI/metadata even when their visual held-charge silhouette is reused. They never inherit another source’s maturity, stored damage or defence eligibility. If a later approved art brief requires a distinct token shape or a genuinely new move, add named FX/pose IDs and revise the counts before generation; do not quietly expand the pack or pretend a missing asset exists.

Stars reuse the chosen action’s body, contact budget and matching authored finite status/effect assets under the manifest’s `starsCompatibility` contract. Their UI icons and progression badges are separate from the 426 slottable ability-icon drafts requested here. Hallowed transformations or future abilities without complete visual contracts remain explicitly unsized future work; no production approval is inferred.

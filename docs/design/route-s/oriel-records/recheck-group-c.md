# Red team group C: pullreading, spark, victory, woodcut (Oriel v6, 32 frames)

All 32 v6 frames were opened at full size, plus the four review sheets. Crops are in
`/mnt/project-files/experiments/route-s-judge-oriel/v6/crops/` (names below). Coordinates are in each frame's own pixels
(x from the left, y from the top). "Length" for a staff is the straight distance from the top of the star finial to the
bottom of the butt cap, measured on grid crops (`*-butt-grid*.png`). For reference, idle 1 (v6) shows about 710 px of staff
(its butt sits behind the boot). Every staff in this group is at or above that.

The art thread's frame check says all 8 frames of all four moves pass. **I disagree for 6 frames:** pullreading 7 and woodcut 3
have a third hand, spark 3 has a staff that no hand holds, woodcut 5 loses the rear arm at impact, and woodcut 1, 7 and 8 are
still a clasp instead of stacked fists.

## Per move

### spark: argue **wire skipping frame 3** (reroll 3 if a skip is not accepted)
- First-ruling faults: the **third hand in 3 is fixed** (two hands now), and the **large white pocket in 6 is fixed**.
- New fault: in 3 the staff hand leaves the staff to make the two-finger sign at her ear, so the staff hangs in the air with no
  hand on it and its butt above the ground. That is a held-not-floating failure, and it is the wrong arm (the brief says the front
  hand draws back). A player sees the staff hang in the air at the wind-up of the move.
- Staff: 814-828 px in every frame (98-100%), straight, star on top. No bendy or short staff.
- Minor: a crimson-pink hair wisp now floats in front of her nose in all 8 frames. At game size it is a few red pixels in front
  of her face and could read as a smear; it is steady (no flicker). The rear-hip book is a brown leather satchel with a star in
  all 8 frames, not the navy book with gold corners. The brief calls a pouch in the book's place a fault; I count it minor because it
  is the same in every frame, but the judge should compare it with the other v6 moves (the navy book is clear in pullreading).
- Skipping 3 leaves 1-2-4-5-6-7-8: raise, release, flick. It still reads as a flick.

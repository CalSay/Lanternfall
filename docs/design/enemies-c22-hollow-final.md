# Hollow: final zone roster proposal

Structure approved; names and balance proposed. Read [the final contract](enemies-c22-final-contract.md). This region has **35 zone monsters, 35 same-design Shadowborn Captain enhancements, seven Champions of Darkness and the Fenmother as Elder of Darkness: 43 unique designs**. Each area appears once, with five consecutive zones. Each zone has five regular fights then its Captain. Area and region bosses are separate one-time encounters. Runtime is unchanged; the owner-approved Thorn Imp art package is linked below.

## Shared card rules

Every creature below was made by or emerged with the darkness. Horn, shell, stone, gills and skeletal shapes are its own anatomy, not a corrupted animal, revived person or possessed item. On defeat its body unravels. Hunting is separate.

Numbers are fixed-zone design targets. HP is ordinary uncritical Attack actions; Speed relative to1.0; armour is physical reduction. Listed weakness/resistance use1.5/0.6, unlisted types neutral. Each normal alternates its two moves. Every hit permits parry or dodge, with the final contract governing per-hit refunds and whole-move counters. Feints never cause damage.

Incoming Bleed and Venom each tick2% reference HP at the next two hero starts. A listed rider occurs once on the specified landed hit; strongest tick refreshes duration, no hidden stacks. Chill is10% Speed reduction for two hero opportunities, max two stacks; Weaken is25% direct-output reduction for one hero opportunity. No rider is implied where none is stated. Enemy Venom remains an explicit runtime approval item.

Captains inherit both moves, anatomy, weakness/resistance and statuses unchanged, plus their listed third move. Captain HP is min(8, normal HP+3) ordinary actions; Speed is normal+0.05, armour unchanged; normal two-consecutive-opportunity cap applies. Captain script1/2/3. No extra generic elite multiplier or trait. Each Captain shares the monster design and pack with a recolour; title and fixed visible marking supplement colour. Any Captain-specific contact poses are planned into that pack upfront. The listed third move supplies its complete damage budget.

Art planning follows the monster design. Seven normal keys is an early estimate, not a limit: every distinct wind-up, contact, charge hold, recovery, hurt or defeat action can receive the poses it needs. Thornwing/Riftwing return to reachable height; multi-limb creatures remain one target. Captain moves below suggest reuse where it reads clearly, but these suggestions do not forbid new Captain-specific poses in the shared character pack. Do not weaken a move to fit an existing frame. Projectiles, weapons and effects are authored alongside the body pack. Champion/Elder key counts below are also estimates; complete pose manifests follow roster and move review.

Rewards: gold, Essence, existing eligible relics. Champions retain existing area Trophy eligibility via coordinator-approved ID mapping. Boss unique policy is still open; no promised chance, guaranteed unique or rematch. No materials. All relative values need joint hero/enemy calibration.

## Seven Champions of Darkness

Each is a unique, one-time area boss after that area's fifth Captain, not an enhanced ordinary monster. They inherit the final charge/control contract: no-damage wind-up, guaranteed hero opportunity,6% actual boss-HP damage or accepted control contribution interrupts; next opportunity recovery, never a second control skip. Every release hit permits either defence. One ordinary move separates charges. Default script1/2/3-charge-release/4; phase changes occur at move end with no free attack. Stats are provisional. Existing area Trophy eligibility maps to these roles; actual item IDs stay with Claude.

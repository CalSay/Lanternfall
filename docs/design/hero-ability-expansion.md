# Full selected-roster ability workshop

Owner request: expand the thirteen selected new heroes, audit/revise them to closure, then independently compare/revise all 34 heroes to closure. Latest priority: maximise fun with nuanced parity between damage, survivability, control and effort. Deliver HTML only; no PDF exports.

- 13 new heroes, 156 signatures: nine actives and three passives each.
- 34 complete kits, 408 signatures, 18 shared class tools and 102 three-slot workshop examples.
- Four optional timed signatures per hero; five untimed actives preserve lower-attention choices.
- Only three cards can be selected. Basic Attack and wholly manual Parry/Dodge remain core actions. No fourth combat ability, unarmed/throwing-weapon kit, party healing or autonomous defence.

Each kit offers a decision about preparation rather than merely escalating a damage number: Slip, a mature Curse, real cooldown availability, Accent, Remedy, colour inscription, fixed Debt, retained Chill, sword Line, Incision, Charge, Attack cadence or Watch/Answer. The passive routes can prepare and spend within their three selected cards; no unselected producer is required. Active routes retain ordinary fallbacks when timing, status or a boss interruption fails.

## Changes to the originals

The independent comparison corrected dead magical armour benefits, passive setup gaps, offensive statuses that expired before ordinary follow-ups, resource prices and starts, held-charge arbitration, queued-arrival precedence, Curse source handling and refund/timing exceptions. Kestrel starts in Long reach; Grenna's attacks explicitly use her held maul. All characters retain their selected identity and existing concept art.

Original large optional direct riders now share a clear aggregate ceiling; printed high-cost base coefficients and sustained engines still require comparison in the real game. Percentage kit bonuses, flat charges and timing are accounted together. Gear, Mark, crit and Star scaling are not silently rewritten. Stored payouts clear their source, receive no second crit/amplification and cannot feed a resource/Star loop.

## Review evidence and limits

[New-hero audit](reviews/new-hero-ability-audit.md): four passes, ending with zero actionable design findings across thirteen kits, 39 routes and thirteen passive-only combinations.

[Independent whole-roster comparison](reviews/whole-roster-ability-audit.md): six structural passes, followed by timing-coverage assessment and a final independent closure check. Changes apply to original and new heroes. The final reviewer reported zero remaining actionable design findings. [Before/after card changes](reviews/whole-roster-changes.json) preserve the revision trail.

[Fun and parity contract](hero-ability-balance-contract.md) defines the complete-fight prototype panel. Damage, surviving imperfect defence, recovery turns, setup burden, resource economy, control and input difficulty must be measured together. Survivability heroes should have meaningful pressure; offensive heroes should have useful safer builds. Neither audit claims numerical equality. Most kits are unimplemented; only Wren, Tobin and Pip are live starters. This work changes design documents and review HTML, not runtime abilities, saves, equipment, approved art or the deployed game.

(Red team critique, Sonnet medium, 2026-10-06 — verbatim summary)
1. Attributes: Vigour is the only attribute that concentrates profitably (pure Vigour ~1.32x win margin vs even at Lv 35, Might ~0.83, Focus ~0.87); casual play collapses to all-Vigour. Fix: soft caps (diminishing past ~50% of points) or two effects per point. Guard is bimodal and a hidden easy mode: +2% counter is overkill on trash / zero for non-parriers; pure Guard at Lv 35 widens parry window +136 ms (76% wider), skill check becomes stat check. Fix: cap Guard window at +60 ms. Might leaks outside turn fights: heroAtk/heroDps feed farmableZone, Deepwell start depth (turnPowerZone(P.A)), parKill, away and raid dps (raiders.dps online), emberBurn — Might applies there with no offset. Fix: apply attrX('atk') to A in turnMakeProfile only. Four attributes fine. Display "+2%" reads as nothing; show totals.
2. Free respec any time lets players counter-pick per fight (Vigour for the boss, Might for trash). Fix: respec at camp only, or pay gold (~one zone-hour per 10 points; also a gold sink), first respec free, or saved loadouts.
3. XP: build's xpNeed is 3.7k/6.0k/9.3k at Lv 30/35/40 vs old 30k/112k/417k (the pacing judge's ease was 33k at Lv 35). Fights per zone to hold the road jump 49% at the zone 35 region step; the road is flattest (0.6 levels/zone) at zones 30-35. Lv 40 base-class cap: levels 41-44 give only base + points, then the Proving jumps Attack ~x1.9 at once (a cliff). Fix: count post-cap levels at half toward the curve. Rubber band: soft ahead of the road, ~2x faster behind; nothing caps deliberate over-levelling.
4. Smoothing: block mean holds (1.011); smoothed is 0.76x stepped at Lv 30/35/40 and 1.32x at 34/39; x2 bend lands at 27 not 30; loses milestone feel at Lv 35/40. Fix: keep smooth, add a small visible fifth-level milestone.
5. Join: a lifted hero owns no tier abilities (Scrolls are spent per hero, replay drop 0.2, pity 5) so the wall moves to Scrolls; prediction 1 must say what kit the switched hero has. Bench XP nearly dead (lift empties the XP bar; only matters above the road). Lift reads S.maxZone not the zone played. Per-hero levels collapse to max(own, road); a new hero arrives with ~108 points and no guidance: add a recommended build / auto-spend.
6. Save key bump not technically needed (S.attr additive, points derived); the flag does not roll back a v6 save (flag on gives an untrained Lv 33 hero). Fix: keep v5 and seed tr to min(L, cap) when the flag flips; or drop "exactly".
7. Gold loses Training (the 'up' sink); gold piles. Fix: don't ship before the gold card, or a priced respec as an interim sink.
8. Measurement: prediction 1 needs a defined attribute policy (all four pure builds plus even) and 100+ fights per arm (30 fights ~7-point SE). Prediction 2 only fails on too slow; add a two-sided band and Tobin 15-30% slower than Wren/Pip. Prediction 3 passes by construction (fights(L) is the knob); add a power-margin check (road-level hero's Attack vs foe HP, zones 20-35). Add a dominance metric: win-rate spread across builds under 15 points at both skills.

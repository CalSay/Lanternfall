# Monetisation research: idle / RPG reference games

Compiled 2026-10-06. Method: WebSearch snippets plus WebFetch. Reddit was skipped. Most wikis and news sites (idleon.wiki, idleon.guide, digitaltq, pcgamer, gamesradar, mmorpg, jagex.com, OSRS wiki, PoE forum, sensortower, games-stats) were blocked by the egress proxy. Only Steam store and Steam community pages could be fetched, so many claims rest on search-engine summaries of those pages. Anything marked **(unverified)** is from a snippet I could not open. Quotes marked "(via fetch summary)" came from a summariser model reading the page, so treat the wording as close but not guaranteed verbatim.

## 1. Legends of IdleOn (key case)

**Dev and model.** Solo dev Lavaflame2 ("Lava"). Free to play on Steam, Android and iOS (iOS since late 2022), cross-platform save. Early access ran about 4 years; the Steam page says it released 6 Nov 2025 ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1476970/Legends_of_IdleOn__Idle_MMO/)). The store page says: "All classes, maps, skills, bosses, and activities are available without purchase" (via fetch summary). Steam reviews: about 75-79% positive of about 21.8k (store page, search snippets).

**What money buys.** There are three layers.

1. **Gem Shop** (gems are the premium currency, also earned free; see below). Gems are sold in packs from $1.99 for 200 gems up to $29.99 for 6,500 gems plus Pet Crystals; the King Doot pack was $9.99 for 2,500 gems (search snippet of idleontoolbox/DigitalTQ, **unverified**, could not open). Shop items are mostly permanent account convenience:
   - Extra card slots. Slots 1-4 cost 150/190/230/270 gems, 840 total, up to 10 slots (search snippet quoting idleon.wiki, **unverified**).
   - Storage chest space and item backpack space (apply to all characters).
   - More food slots, carry capacity, a 3D-printer slot, and an Anvil upgrade.
   - Chat rings, which are cosmetic. A guide calls them "pretty much cosmetic items, with the only reason to buy them being to support the game" (search summary of the Steam Gem Shop Guide).
   - Pristine Charm: a random Pristine Charm delivered into the Sneaking inventory. Players complained it was too scarce, so Lava boosted the free find chance (search snippet quoting the wiki 2025 changelog, **unverified**).
   - Guides rank card slots, storage, backpack space, food slots and carry capacity as "amazing or great value". They say Worlds 1-2 are "pretty free to play friendly" and recommend buying card slots and storage "on an as-needed basis".
2. **Weekly or limited bundles** (real money). Only one bundle runs per week. Example: Gilded Treasure Pack, $19.99, with 4,500 gems, a cosmetic item, 15 event plays and Prisma bubbles (search snippet quoting the wiki changelog, **unverified**). Players report "$30 bundles with useful items" and some report weekly bundles over $50 (search summaries). Bundles carry stat bonuses such as "+30% afk gains" and capes. One Steam poster says bundles are "mostly capes with better stats than boss capes" (via fetch summary of [p2w or f2p thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/599648870459309541/?l=english&ctp=2)).
3. **Companions (pets) gacha**, added June 2023. Pet Crystals are bought with real money. The best pet, King Doot ("All Divinities from World 5 count as Active"), started at 0.28% to 0.85% per pull. Players say that for free players it meant 38 treats plus 200 pity pulls, about 238 weeks (search snippet, **unverified**). Lava later added a pity guarantee (guaranteed after 200 treated attempts). Free pets arrive 1 per week (the early complaint) and later as free daily pets, a "Pet Mart+" with 19 rotating daily options, and a free "Pet Bonus Token" that Lava called "my thanks to you for playing my game!" (via fetch summary of [Steam news](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/allnews/)). This is a partial retreat after the backlash.

**The $5 autoloot.** Auto-loot is real-money only, about $5, and is a recurring flashpoint. Verbatim from [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/3803901559418465265/) (via fetch summary):
- "If the game was worth $5 then put that price on the game and stop with these cancer microtransactions."
- Another player says it "didn't exist for the first year or two of the game" and that "$5 is really cheap compared to other 'free' games".
- A second thread: "The one and only thing that you need to buy (only with real money) is auto-loot, about 5$", and "It is pay to convenience in most cases... you do not need it right away at all." ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/4146194159904171286), via fetch summary).

**Free gems.** Gems can be earned free, but slowly.
- A player gets "around 800-1000 a week and i do not nearly do everything", while new players get about 30 a day. Gems "almost pop every time you gain levels" later in the game ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/601898034735905546/)).
- Free sources:
  - Hourly Spiketrap minigame, 2-40 gems a day.
  - Guild weekly gift boxes.
  - Seasonal events, "80~100 free gems daily" in the summer event.
  - Lava's Twitch streams.
  - Alchemy shop, about 10 a day.
- The same thread says faster gem methods were "severely nerfed" over time.
- Guides say it is technically possible to buy everything free, but "getting free gems is very slow" (search summary).

**Character slots.** I could not confirm that character slots are sold for gems. Search results indicate slots unlock by account or class level (about 10-11 slots by May 2026; thresholds quoted as 20/30/70/150/300/500/750/1100/1500 total levels), with a 12th planned (**unverified**, conflicting snippets). A 2021-era thread says the cap was 9 then. Do not model IdleOn as "character-slot monetisation"; the shop sells card, storage, bag and food slots instead.

**How players view the gem shop.**
- Majority framing is "pay to skip the grind" / "Pay 2 Progress" / "PAY-2-CONVENIENCE".
  - "There is nothing to 'win', it's basically endless progression idle game with no PvP whatsoever." ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/5590709039476764769/), via fetch summary).
  - "Can something be pay to win if there is no win state?" (paraphrase in same summaries).
  - "I've spent nothing on the game and have had fun playing it the last month... I've never felt pressure to buy anything." ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/599648870459309541/?l=english&ctp=2), via fetch summary).
  - "The game is perfectly playable without buying any of it." ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/3049482570181461216/)).
  - Some players buy to "support the developer".
- Complaint side is about escalation, not the base shop:
  - "Monetisation bundles still exist and provide strong bonuses unavailable to F2P players" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/5590709039476764769/), via fetch summary).
  - The King Doot pet "affects so many systems and removes all sorts of meaningful thought" (same thread).
  - A recent Steam review: "the pay to win in this game gets worse with literally every update... power crept like crazy" (search snippet, **unverified**).
  - A critic calls it "pay-to-have-fun freemium mobile quality game with every single aspect tied to the cash shop" (search snippet, **unverified**).
  - Another post complains of "frequent pop-up advertisements for purchasable content" and a pet tournament seen as favouring spenders ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/805719856669439361/), via fetch summary).

**Gacha controversy detail** ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/5514142341097797009/), via fetch summary):
- "1 free pet follower per week feels pretty selfish, especially when compared to the rest of the game that is totally free beside this."
- "going from mocking maplestory, to becoming maplestory, to becoming more predatory than maplestory."
- Players say they were banned from the official Discord for calling it predatory.
- A claim that Apple rules make raw gacha without a mercy mechanic hard on iOS (**unverified**).
- Search summaries say Lava "admitted he messed up" after over a year but did not unban anyone, and that the dev explained pay-to-win-style updates as marketing spend to bring the game to more people ([gamingtierlist](https://www.gamingtierlist.com/news/idleon-developer-claims-pay2win-update-is-for-marketing/), blocked, **unverified**).

**Lava reputation.** Mixed. Respected for the one-person build, huge free content depth and free-gem handouts on stream. Resented for the gacha, the Discord bans, escalating bundles ("Dev lied again" and "Wow such greed" thread titles exist). The Steam "more predatory stuff thanks Lava" thread title shows the tone.

**Revenue scale.** A Steam forum post cites a third-party estimate of about $1M per month gross, and a daily peak near $100k in early 2024, top-30 Steam seller then ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/805719856669439361/); original source is games-stats/Sensor Tower style estimates, **unverified**). Search snippets cite about 856k Steam units and Google Play 5M+ installs (**unverified**). The Steam/mobile revenue split was not found.

**Same dev, premium sibling.** Lava's other games: Fortune Mill is $5.99 one-time with "NO microtransactions" (Steam news, via fetch summary). Idle Skilling is a free-to-play game with a gem shop (search snippet).

**Time-savers players accept vs resent (IdleOn synthesis).**
- Accepted: one-off permanent QoL such as auto-loot at $5 (grumbled at, but widely defended), storage and bag space, card slots, and free gems that let you buy the same things slowly.
- Resented: power that can't be earned (limited-time pets, exclusive bundle bonuses), FOMO rotations, repeating $20-50 bundles, and silencing critics.

## 2. Melvor Idle

- **Model:** premium. Steam base game $9.99 list; during the fetch it showed $3.99 on sale. Expansions Into the Abyss, Atlas of Discovery and Throne of the Herald are $4.99 each ($2.99 on sale). Bundles $6.49 and $12.96 on sale. Melvor Idle 2 is $14.99 (search snippet). Mobile is free to try with a one-time $9.99 unlock (search snippet).
- **Cosmetics:** only via Steam Points Shop (pets, profile backgrounds).
- **Praise:** "Melvor Idle does not contain microtransactions. We believe everyone who plays should be on a level playing field." ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1267910/Melvor_Idle/)). Steam 92% positive of 8.6k.
- **Complaints:** none about monetisation found. Jagex published it; a Feb 2024 Jagex sale to investors reportedly paused publishing talks and Melvor 2 is self-published (search snippet, **unverified**).
- Patreon $5/month exists as support (search snippet).

## 3. Old School RuneScape (and RuneScape 3 as contrast)

- **OSRS model:** subscription ("membership") plus Bonds. Bonds are tradeable items bought with real money that give 14 days of membership or can be sold for gold. Jagex's line: aside from bonds there are no microtransactions in OSRS, no Treasure Hunter, Squeal of Fortune or Solomon's store ([OSRS wiki via search](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:Bonds_and_RuneFest)). Bonds sit on the Grand Exchange at about 12.6M gp (search snippet).
- **Prices:** reports say that on 10 March 2026 membership rose from $11.99 to $14.99 a month ($131.88 a year) and a bond from $6.99 to $9.99, with bulk bonds at $29.95 for 3 and $192.99 for 20; subscribers from before 27 Sep 2024 are grandfathered (search snippets from osrslegends/osrsguru, **unverified**). A separate Sep 2024 rise is reported as $12.49 to $13.99 a month (a Yahoo/search snippet that appears to describe RS3; the two sources conflict, **unverified**).
- **2019 partnership polls (the "microtransactions via a third party" backlash):** Jagex closed polls early in July 2019 at about 70% opposed. Jagex: "You came to vote in your thousands, and the voting so far has sent a clear message" ([PCGamesN via search](https://www.pcgamesn.com/old-school-runescape/microtransactions)). I did not find a 2023-24 OSRS monetisation poll; the Sep 2024 Summer Summit mega poll (121,597 votes) was content, not money (search snippet). The 2023-24 polls you mentioned **could not be verified**.
- **RS3 reckoning (most relevant to our design):**
  - Jan 2025: a Jagex survey floated an "ads included" tier and a $350/year tier. Players cancelled subscriptions in protest ([Massively OP via search](https://massivelyop.com/2025/01/17/runescape-fans-riot-over-jagex-survey-on-33-subs-in-game-ads-and-account-restrictions/)).
  - June 2025: CEO Jon Bellamy: "Our MTX approach is harming RuneScape, and it's time that we took action" (search snippet quoting multiple outlets).
  - Treasure Hunter was paused in July 2025.
  - A poll in Oct-Nov 2025 needed 100,000 votes and finished with 124,985 votes.
  - Treasure Hunter was removed 19 Jan 2026, and Jagex cut direct-XP items, Proteans, Dummies and other "gameplay-impacting MTX".
  - Reported cost: RS3 revenue fell about 40% (YouTube short headline, **unverified**; Bellamy had said Jagex "probably won't maintain the revenue that we currently are").
- **Lesson:** time-for-money trades (bonds) are tolerated because the buyer pays real money and the seller gets gold, with no new power created. Sold XP and loot-box power are what players voted out.

## 4. Idle Slayer

- **Model:** free to play with rewarded ads and a "Jewels of Soul"/gems shop on mobile. Steam used to be $4.99 premium and is now free with IAP, still Mostly Positive (78% of 4.3k) ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1353300/Idle_Slayer/)).
- **Sells:** cosmetic characters (Haskel etc.), permanent upgrades (souls, bag capacity, CPS, armory chance), temporary boosts, ad removal.
- **Fair vs not:** ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1353300/discussions/0/824829635640469034/), via fetch summary)
  - "Avoid the time limited boosts, they are not worth it at all."
  - "Never buy temp bonus's, ever."
  - Ad removal at about $8 felt overpriced.
  - Permanent upgrades and cosmetics are seen as fair, and purchases as support.

## 5. Soda Dungeon 2

- **Model:** F2P with ads. IAP range to $49.99, two types: Bundles and "Caps". Ad removal is a purchase.
- **Fair:** all IAPs can also be bought with in-game gold. Quote: "All in-app purchases can be purchased with in-game gold. So you could not spend a dime and still have everything." ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/946050/discussions/0/2565312892656698392/), via fetch summary). Examples: 2x-speed clock about $3-4 or about 500M gold; 200 caps about $2.
- **Complaints:** forced ads after dungeons, crashes on revive-ads, a bundle lost after a dimension reset ([Drinking Buddies thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/946050/discussions/0/5386857950348690425/), title only).

## 6. Shop Titans

- **Model:** F2P, gems, timers, reward rolls, perk bundles, and a roughly $10/month subscription that makes repairs cheaper and adds loot. Cosmetic DLC on Steam.
- **Reception:** harsh on Steam. Metacritic user reviews 54% negative, 15% positive (search snippet, **unverified**).
- **Quotes** ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1258080/discussions/0/2946998508803954809/), via fetch summary): "Every broken item costs around half million..."; "at higher levels, you do pay or leave"; a subscriber "made over x30 the money as a free 2 play". Defenders: "There is no PVP here"; "You can get everything just by putting in the time".
- A review headline: "A Cool Idea Ruined By Freemium Mechanics" (GuruGamer, search).
- **Unfair pattern:** friction invented to sell the fix (item breakage, timers), plus a subscription that multiplies earnings.

## 7. Leaf Blower Revolution

- **Model:** free to play on Steam and mobile, with DLC packs. Steam: Premium Pack $24.99, Cards and Borbventure $13.99 each, Materials $8.99, Supporter $5.99, Skins $3.99, Soundtrack $4.99, all $76.93. Mobile has gem packs ($2.99-11.99), Supporter $4.99, Premium $20.99 and ads (search snippets).
- **Reception:** 94% positive of 22,605 ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1468260/Leaf_Blower_Revolution__Idle_Game/)). Best-liked pricing in this set: DLC packs bought once, supporter pack, skins and soundtrack.

## 8. NGU Idle

- **Model:** free to play on Steam, IAP plus passively earned premium currency. Store text: "Premium currency you can PASSIVELY OBTAIN at a decent pace, without paying a cent." 95% positive of 10.9k ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1147690/NGU_IDLE/)).
- Players: in-game purchases exist "in a very non-aggressive way... all in-game purchase options seem to simply be Quality of Life upgrades" (search snippet quoting Steam reviews).

## 9. Orna

- **Model:** F2P GPS RPG by Northern Forge Studios. Claims no stamina, no gacha/loot boxes, no pay-to-win ("survived and thrived for eight years" without them; Games Press release via search). Patreon supports development.
- **Store IAP, USD:** Shrine of Luck/Wisdom $0.99, Arena Tokens $0.99, Faction Reset $0.99, Gauntlet Keys $1.99, Starter Pack $2.99, Galar's Hammers $2.99, Latchstone $4.99, Promotional Pack (L) $9.99 (Apple/Google listings, search snippet). Mostly small convenience items.
- Reception detail not verified beyond the studio's own press claims.

## 10. AFK Arena / AFK Journey

- **Model:** F2P gacha with hero summons, limited-time bundles (reported 2-hour windows), battle pass and seasonal PvP.
- **Complaints:** "insanely predatory", heavy FOMO, "the endgame competitive scene is pay-to-win", free rewards nerfed per season ([MMORPG review snippet](https://www.mmorpg.com/reviews/afk-journey-review-heroes-guilds-and-gacha-should-you-invest-your-time-2000131159)). Praise: 60 free launch pulls and generous login rewards.
- Not a model for us; it is the cautionary case.

## 11. Path of Exile (1 and 2)

- **Model:** F2P. Everything is cosmetic except storage and slots. Premium stash tab $1.50 (15 points), bundle 200 points about $20 for six tabs, often on sale; character slots sold too. Supporter packs $30 to $480 (PoE 2), whose points buy stash tabs, character slots or cosmetics. Packs are upgradeable (pay $30, later $30 more for the $60 pack).
- **Fair:** widely held as among the best F2P models. "There is no direct player power tied behind spending real money."
- **Contested:** stash tabs. The "Convince me Stash tab MTX is not pay to win" forum thread says trading effectively needs premium tabs. Defenders say you can play self-found without them, "can get everything needed for about $50" ([PlayerAuctions blog via search](https://blog.playerauctions.com/path-of-exile/is-path-of-exile-pay-to-win-a-complete-guide-on-whats-free-and-what-isnt/)). PoE 2 cosmetic prices drew anger, with full skin packs around $76 (search snippet).

## 12. Warframe (fairness reference)

- **Model:** F2P. Platinum is the premium currency. It is buyable or earned by trading items with other players. Sells cosmetics, slots, boosters, Prime Access bundles with Platinum bonuses, and TennoGen community cosmetics.
- Gear is obtainable free. "Every piece of gear... can be obtained without spending a single penny" (search summary). Estimated $150-200M a year (**unverified**).
- Steam: Very Positive, 600k+ reviews, 88% positive recently (search snippet). Community often calls it "one of the most fair F2P games".
- **Known complaints:** Prime Access and heirloom bundle prices ("the worst predatory thing DE has done so far"; Sportskeeda headline, search) and inventory slot pressure.

## 13. Brighter Shores (Fen Research, RuneScape creator Andrew Gower)

- **Model:** F2P with a Premium Pass: $5.99 per 30 days, $23.99 per 90 days, $74.99 per 365 days (Deltias Gaming via search). Free tier gets the first two episodes; premium unlocks later episodes, more professions and areas, name changes, and player trading. No shop.
- Dev quote on Steam: "There's nothing you can buy from any P2W shops, there's no shop." ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2791440/discussions/0/601896872885360957/), via fetch summary). No P2W complaints in that thread; one player praised independent funding.
- Early Access, reviews Mixed (67% of 8.6k, recent mostly negative). The criticism is about game content, not money.

## 14. Industry benchmarks

- **Payer conversion (F2P mobile):**
  - Unity's 2024 report reportedly puts it at about 1.83% ([search summary](https://maf.ad/en/blog/mobile-game-conversion-rates/), page blocked, **unverified**).
  - Industry rule of thumb is 1-5%, with 0.8% in some 2025 benchmarks.
  - 50% of revenue comes from about 0.2% of players.
  - ARPPU about $43 and ARPU about $2-3 (aggregator figures, **unverified**; they cite no primary source).
- **Revenue mix:**
  - One 2025 summary says idle games earn 54.7% from IAP, 28.3% from ads and 12.6% from premium price. Another says idle mobile earns 60-70% from ads and 30-40% from IAP. These contradict each other and are low-quality aggregators, so treat as **unverified**.
  - Cosmetics are cited as nearly 80% of revenue only for competitive/social titles (Fortnite, Roblox, LoL), not idle games.
  - I found **no public cosmetics-vs-convenience split for indie idle RPGs**.
- **Indie data point:** IdleOn's reported scale (about $1M a month estimate) came mostly from convenience, bundles and a gacha, not cosmetics (inference from what is sold; the split is not published).

## 15. Patterns across the set

| Seen as fair | Seen as predatory |
|---|---|
| One-time permanent QoL (autoloot, storage, speed clock) at low price | Time-limited boosts and temp bonuses (Idle Slayer) |
| Premium price up front, no MTX (Melvor, Fortune Mill) | Gacha for power with tiny odds and FOMO rotation (IdleOn pets, AFK Journey) |
| Everything purchasable also earnable in-game (Soda Dungeon 2, NGU) | Friction invented to sell the fix (Shop Titans breakage) |
| Cosmetics, supporter packs, soundtrack (Leaf Blower, PoE, Warframe) | Recurring weekly bundles with exclusive stat bonuses (IdleOn) |
| Membership that unlocks content, not power (Brighter Shores, OSRS) | Sold XP and loot boxes (RS3 Treasure Hunter, removed after poll) |
| Free currency drip so non-payers can buy shop items (IdleOn gems, NGU) | Silencing critics (IdleOn Discord bans) |

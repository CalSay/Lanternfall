Meet your next main.

Twenty concept profiles. Nineteen eligible candidates. Eleven open places. Looking for a meaningful connection with your three ability slots.

Unapproved design candidates; no gameplay or approved artwork changes. Rankings are design judgement, not combat measurements. No candidate is approved or integrated.

Who’s already at the party?

21 revised hero candidates: 8 Warriors · 5 Rangers · 8 Mages. Expanded kits are proposals; Wren, Tobin and Pip are the implemented starters.

Most convincing gaps

Dedicated Curse, Dodge-led follow-up, offensive timing, cooldown investment, cleanse/prevention, and visible finishing windows. Existing riders do not yet make these a complete hero identity.

Crowded territory

Poison, frost, Ward/Guard retention, armour opening, prepared burst and delayed impacts already have several candidates. New entrants here must justify a different action decision; they rank lower when they mainly change presentation.

Roster shape

Current proposals: 8 Warriors, 5 Rangers, 8 Mages. Top 11 add 2 Warriors, 3 Rangers and 6 Mages, producing 10 / 8 / 14. This is a playstyle-first shortlist, not a class-parity target; lower-ranked bow and melee alternatives remain available.

Honest scope

Only Wren, Tobin and Pip are implemented starters. This audit uses the revised 21 design candidates, including proposed Brynja Doorward, not 21 production-ready kits. All 20 additions are proposals. NPCs, benched designs and the rejected bard direction are not recycled.

Foundation rules

One hero, one foe, Speed timeline. Attack generates the hero resource. Every enemy hit still uses manual Parry/Dodge. Three selected slots include passives. No parties, automatic counters from passives, free turns, movement lanes, summons or thrown physical props. Every hero carries a lantern; Mage staff+tome compatibility remains a gear migration.

Build scope

Each profile gives a concrete three-slot loadout and an alternative route. Most illustrate two actives plus a passive; Peregrine uses three actives to demonstrate cooldown transfer. These are concept slices, not complete unlock trees or validated full-passive builds. Complete costs, cooldowns, Stars, talents and boss simulations follow only after selection.

Nerys once carried medicine through a village whose doors opened only to strangers. She learned to leave before gratitude became a trap. Now she returns for the people she left behind.

You’ll recognise me by…

A wiry woman in her forties, long nose, dark cropped curls and a russet split riding coat. Plain shortbow, fox-brass belt lantern, sturdy boots; no dangling tails.

Combat gear: Bow + quiver. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: dodge follow-up

Attack earns resource. A fully manual Dodge banks one Slip; the next chosen shot spends it for penetration or protection. Attack remains useful when no Slip is available.

It’s complicated:

Spend the earned Slip on a hard shot, or keep it for a safer recovery. A new Slip replaces the old one; it never stacks.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Foxstep Shot · ActiveSpend one Slip for a penetrating arrow; without Slip, a normal useful shot.
  • Homeward Turn · ActiveSpend resource to gain brief Guard and clear one eligible slow or Chill; consumes Slip for a stronger recovery instead of damage.
  • Light Feet · PassivePassive: fully Dodge every hit of one enemy move to bank one Slip, once per move. No automatic evasion or countershot.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Foxstep Shot / Homeward Turn / Parting Arrow (a paid shot that primes one Slip after it resolves). Trade the passive for deliberate preparation.

Before we commit

New Slip token and hero kit; existing gear families.

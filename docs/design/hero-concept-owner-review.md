# Owner review of twenty concept drafts

Branch: `codex/hero-concept-owner-review`. Art is unchanged; this records review and disposition.

The numbering below follows generation release/completion order, not the ranked profiles. The original image timestamps and named comments cross-check the mapping. There are nineteen eligible concepts after Rufus is withdrawn; two of those are deferred behind crafting, and Petra needs kit review. This does not change the existing twenty-one hero dossier.

## Current constraints

- No unarmed playable characters: Rufus is withdrawn, with the concept preserved as an archive.
- Ludo and Maud wait for equipment/crafting work. No one-character new gear pair will be added as a stopgap.
- Every character retains a lantern. Mage staff+tome remains the proposed compatibility direction.
- Broaden ethnic, age and facial diversity in future character work. Peregrine specifically needs tightly coiled African-origin hair texture and a matching textured beard; keep his elderly identity.
- Liked designs take precedence over earlier reviewer taste. In particular, preserve Sable’s exceptional visual direction. Positive concept feedback does not grant whole-pack or runtime approval.

## Petra’s kit review

Petra is intended as a committed heavy-shot archer: use a hero action to prepare a finite Draw, then spend it on a heavy arrow or abandon it for protection. Wren instead manages Mark, precision and wounds; Nerys centres on manually earned Dodge choices. However, Petra’s current prepare/release slice overlaps several existing kits and is not yet sufficiently distinctive. Keep her visual concept; require a concrete new three-slot decision and useful unprepared Attack route before promoting her. No numeric tuning, replacement kit or gameplay implementation is approved by this review.

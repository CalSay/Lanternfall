A little about me

Celandine studied wounds by lamplight after the battle surgeons left. She now seeks the source of the dark that keeps opening old scars.

You’ll recognise me by…

A middle-aged woman with deep-set eyes, dark olive skin and closely clipped grey hair. Clean pale gold coat with broad black cuffs, slender split-circle staff and red medical tome; amber lantern.

Combat gear: Staff + anatomical tome. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: precision holy damage

Attack earns resource. Place one Incision through a holy spell. Preserve it for low-pressure penetration or close it with a stronger strike and self-recovery.

It’s complicated:

Recovery is bounded per release and competes with damage; no damage-to-heal percentage exploit or automatic resurrection.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

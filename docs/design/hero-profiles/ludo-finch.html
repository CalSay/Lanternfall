A little about me

Ludo kept a watchtower alone until the signal fires answered with the wrong colours. He took its smallest windlass and went looking for the true keepers.

You’ll recognise me by…

A short sturdy man in his sixties with a broad face, ruddy skin and thick white eyebrows. Intact dark green watch coat, compact crossbow and square shuttered lantern.

Combat gear: Combat crossbow + bolt case (future gear). Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: reload and prepared volleys

Attack always remains usable and earns resource. A paid Load prepares one Volley token; spend it for a pooled multi-bolt action or trade it for protection.

It’s complicated:

Reload is a chosen ability action, never a hidden restriction on normal Attack. Multiple bolts share a single grade and proc budget.

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

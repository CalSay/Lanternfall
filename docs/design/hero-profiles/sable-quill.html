Design direction retained

Owner note #8 in release order: Wow Sable Quill looks exceptional! Big fan of this one.

Preserve the strongly liked face, colours and costume. Earlier reviewer suggestions do not override this direct feedback; any ledger/focus change must preserve the identity the owner likes.

Art review notes

Reads younger than the intended fifties. Ringed focus became a fire-orb apparatus and the tome gained Pip-like leaf ornament instead of a ledger motif.

A little about me

Sable was a plague-house clerk who remembered every name the town tried to erase. Her cures have become sharper since the town stopped asking politely.

You’ll recognise me by…

A tall, angular woman in her fifties, olive skin, severe straight nose and tightly bound black-and-white hair. Cream high-collared coat with plum sleeves; ringed staff, ledger tome and brass lantern.

Combat gear: Staff + remedy tome. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: debuff recovery and reversal

Attack earns resource. A paid remedy clears one eligible harmful status. If one was actually removed, bank one Remedy; spend it on a later spell or protective seal.

It’s complicated:

Against a clean foe, deliberate preparation still supplies a smaller Remedy route. Clearing harmless effects cannot farm tokens.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Read the Fever · ActivePaid cleanse of one eligible self-debuff; successful removal banks one Remedy. If none exists, gain modest Ward instead. With no removable status, choose before payment between modest Ward or a smaller prepared Remedy; either spends the action and full stated cost. No same-action cleanse and preparation.
  • Bitter Cure · ActivePaid spell; consume Remedy for added direct damage or Weaken, choosing one.
  • Clean Hands · PassivePassive: Attack may spend Remedy for brief Ward after contact instead of carrying it for a spell.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Read the Fever / Bitter Cure / Prepare the Salve (a non-damaging paid preparation that banks Remedy without needing to be afflicted).

Before we commit

Eligible status list, Remedy provenance and mage tome migration. No mandatory taking damage.

Why I ranked this match here

Adds a missing self-recovery specialist who still wins fights; unlike a party healer, all decisions serve the solo hero.

A little about me

Ione restored stained windows in a chapel with no congregation. The colours now remember the sunlight more faithfully than the sky does.

You’ll recognise me by…

A short woman in her thirties, East Asian features, strong brows and blunt navy-black bob. White-and-cobalt intact coat, triangular glass staff head; red lantern and folded colour-chart tome.

Combat gear: Staff + prism tome. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: element sequencing

Attack earns resource. Choose a fire or cold inscription. A later opposite-element active may consume it for a bounded mixed strike, or replace it with the new element. Repeating the element retains it and grants brief non-stacking Ward instead of a mixed rider.

It’s complicated:

Only one inscription exists. Mixing sacrifices the defensive reserve; no recursive reactions or extra action.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Red Window · ActivePaid fire spell. With an opening cold inscription, choose to consume it for bounded mixed direct power or replace it with fire. With opening fire, retain it and gain brief non-stacking Ward. With none, establish fire. All branches use one action budget.
  • Blue Window · ActivePaid cold spell, symmetrically: consume opening fire for mixed direct power or replace it with cold; repeat opening cold for brief non-stacking Ward; establish cold when none exists. It does not apply Freeze.
  • Leadwork · PassivePassive: an Attack while an inscription exists may clear it for brief Ward after contact.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Red Window / Blue Window / Close the Shutters (clear inscription for stronger Ward and eligible cleanse).

Before we commit

Inscription and symmetric fire/cold branch rules; mage tome migration.

Why I ranked this match here

Compelling three-slot puzzle and clean art; Pip already has cold conversion, so elemental novelty alone is insufficient.

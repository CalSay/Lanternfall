A little about me

Eamon challenged a toll collector and won the bridge. He has spent the years since learning that winning a crossing is easier than keeping it open.

You’ll recognise me by…

A lanky man in his thirties with a crooked smile, red-brown skin and cropped auburn hair. Clean sand-coloured coat, slim warblade, small plain shield and bridge-brass lantern.

Combat gear: Warblade + shield. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: offensive sequence variation

Attack earns resource. A cut primes High or Low; choose a different next active for penetration, or repeat the line for Guard.

It’s complicated:

High and Low are action tags, not reach or movement controls. Only one line can be primed; every response uses a hero turn.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • High Cut · ActivePaid sword blow that primes High.
  • Low Answer · ActivePaid sword blow: consume High for penetration, or prime Low if no High exists.
  • Same Road · PassivePassive: snapshot the opening Line before an active cut. If its tag repeats that Line, choose to consume the old Line for brief Guard after contact; choosing Guard suppresses that action’s re-priming. A first cut with no Line cannot qualify.

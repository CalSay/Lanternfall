A little about me

Flint maintained the road’s lightning rods. When the lamps went dark, he discovered the storm was still answering somewhere beneath the ground.

You’ll recognise me by…

A stocky middle-aged man with a broken nose and tightly curled silver hair. Navy work coat, broad copper cuffs, straight forked conductor staff; caged lantern and blue circuit tome.

Combat gear: Staff + conductor tome. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: lightning and charge-break planning

Attack charges a small reserve. Choose an immediate bolt or reserve charge for a stronger later strike. Against a gathering boss, the paid strike contributes real damage to the existing break threshold.

It’s complicated:

An immediate bolt wins ordinary pressure. The paid grounded branch instead spends the entire reserve on a bounded gathering-phase strike and subsequent brief Ward, sacrificing future bolts; it must still meet real damage/control break rules, with no guaranteed interrupt.

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

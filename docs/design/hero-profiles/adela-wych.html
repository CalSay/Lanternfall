A little about me

Adela settles the debts of people buried without their names. She will not bring a soul back; she will make the living acknowledge what they owe.

You’ll recognise me by…

A statuesque woman in her sixties, warm brown skin, pronounced cheekbones and short white hair. Clean black-and-pearl ceremonial coat, bone-white staff finial shaped as a closed arch; violet lantern.

Combat gear: Staff + testament tome. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: finite death-magic debt

Attack earns resource. A paid death spell records a bounded Debt from its own direct hit. Collect it with a later spell, or cancel it for Ward.

It’s complicated:

Debt stores a fixed authored amount, never a percentage of arbitrary incoming damage, and cannot record its own collection.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Name the Debt · ActiveDirect spell that records one bounded Debt, replacing the previous one.
  • Final Account · ActiveConsume Debt for a single additional damage rider; no repeat crit or recording that rider.
  • Merciful Ledger · PassivePassive: Attack may cancel existing Debt for a small eligible self-cleanse, forfeiting the stored damage; no cancellation benefit when no eligible debuff exists. No additional Attack or Debt creation.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Name the Debt / Final Account / Lay to Rest (consume Debt for Ward; works for lesser protection without it).

Before we commit

New finite Debt token and mage tome migration; no summons or life-drain immortality.

Why I ranked this match here

Distinct death-magic presentation with bounded accounting, but competes with Mab for a finite delayed-payoff place. Rank eleven is the least certain shortlist pick.

Design direction retained

Owner note #2 in release order: Love it. Great design. Made me realise how we need more different race characters.

Preserve the liked spindle/thread design. Broaden ethnicity and facial diversity across future additions; do not recolour existing approved characters to satisfy a quota.

Art review notes

Spindle and stitch identity works. Broad build could be more pronounced; lantern and gold trim are more ornate than intended.

A little about me

Mab repaired mourning clothes until the dead began leaving instructions in their hems. She does not raise them. She finishes the promises they cannot.

You’ll recognise me by…

A broad older woman with a hooked nose, deep brown skin and a single white braid. Indigo fitted coat, ivory seam markings, spindle-shaped staff; amber lantern and stitched black tome.

Combat gear: Staff + hex tome. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: curse and delayed judgement

Attack builds resource. Bind one visible Curse to the foe; after one complete foe move it ripens. Cut it for damage, or unpick it to weaken the next move.

It’s complicated:

An early release works but loses the mature payoff. Only one Curse may exist; replacing it discards its age.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Last Stitch · ActiveApply or replace the single Curse; it matures after a resolved foe move, not each hit.
  • Cut the Thread · ActiveConsume the Curse for direct damage, or choose Weaken instead; mature Curse strengthens the selected result.
  • Patient Hands · PassivePassive: the first natural maturation of each newly authored Curse grants one resource; extensions and replacements do not count.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Last Stitch / Cut the Thread / Unpick (consume Curse to clear one eligible self-debuff and gain brief Ward).

Before we commit

Curse timing/ownership must be specified; mage tome compatibility migration.

Why I ranked this match here

Adds a genuinely absent hex specialist with a readable setup and two different releases; no minions required.

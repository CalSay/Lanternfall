My love language: declared heavy-shot commitment

Attack earns resource. Spend an action preparing one Draw. Your next chosen shot spends it for a larger hit, or you abandon it to recover protection.

It’s complicated:

Readiness is a stored token between turns, never a held real-time channel during the foe’s move. Delaying cannot grow it infinitely.

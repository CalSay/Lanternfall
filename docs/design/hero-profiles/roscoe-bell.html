A little about me

Roscoe was ordered to close the granary during a famine. He broke its lock instead. Now every locked door reminds him that obedience is also a choice.

You’ll recognise me by…

A massive Black man in his forties, broad cheekbones, shaved head and clipped beard. Ochre quilted coat, battered steel shield, wide warblade and square iron lantern.

Combat gear: Warblade + shield. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: aggressive guard breaking

Attack earns resource. Establish one Breach through a paid blow; spend it to cut through armour, or use it to weaken the foe while keeping normal offence.

It’s complicated:

Breach is your visible setup, not a promise that every enemy has removable buffs. Boss protection is never deleted wholesale.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

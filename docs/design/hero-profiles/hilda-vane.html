A little about me

Hilda once hunted to feed a besieged hamlet. She still leaves a portion for the next traveller, but has stopped offering mercy to the things that wear stolen faces.

You’ll recognise me by…

A broad strong woman in her fifties with a wide nose, dark skin and close silver curls. Cream padded hunting coat, brick-red scarf, sturdy bow and oven-glass hip lantern.

Combat gear: Bow + quiver. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: enemy-health execution window

Attack earns resource. A paid shot establishes one Chance. Spend it immediately for reliable penetration, or hold it until the foe is visibly below a declared health threshold for a stronger direct finish.

It’s complicated:

The same finite Chance pays for either branch. Waiting may mean spending several turns without the payoff; the finish never kills automatically and works above the threshold.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Find the Seam · ActivePaid arrow that establishes one Chance, replacing the old one.
  • Last Mercy · ActiveConsume Chance for a penetrating arrow, or choose a larger direct rider only if the foe begins the action below the proposed 35% health window. The threshold is provisional tuning, not measured balance.
  • Enough for One · PassiveAttack may consume Chance for modest direct power instead of waiting for Last Mercy. No on-kill income or extra Attack.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Find the Seam / Last Mercy / Spare Breath (consume Chance for brief Guard rather than the finish).

Before we commit

New Chance token and explicit start-of-action health snapshot; existing bow gear. Threshold and damage need real-core tuning.

Why I ranked this match here

Adds a missing visible finishing-window choice without instant kills or a kit that only works after victory.

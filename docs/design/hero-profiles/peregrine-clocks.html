A little about me

Peregrine repaired the village clock until it began striking for people who had not yet died. He dismantled it, kept one spring, and went to find who had wound it.

You’ll recognise me by…

A thin older man with a long face, deep brown skin and short tightly coiled silver hair and a close, textured silver beard. Prussian-blue tailored work coat, straight staff with one enclosed clock dial, indexed brass tome and amber pocket lantern.

Combat gear: Staff + watchmaker tome. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: cooldown investment

Attack earns resource. A paid preparation delays one selected equipped active by a turn to shorten a different equipped active by a turn. Or use the preparation for a bounded direct strike without altering cooldowns.

It’s complicated:

A transfer costs an action, resource and real future availability from an already-cooling donor. Attack resets the transfer lock. Passives, ready skills and unequipped cards cannot be targets.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Borrow Tomorrow · ActivePaid preparation: choose two different other equipped active skills, both already cooling. Add one cooldown turn to the donor and remove one from the recipient. Cannot target itself. One transfer until a normal Attack intrinsically resets the lock. Actually delaying Clockstrike creates one replaceable Receipt. If no legal pair exists, choose a paid next-Attack penetration fallback before payment; it creates no Receipt.
  • Clockstrike · ActiveUseful paid direct spell. If Borrow Tomorrow delayed it, its next resolved use may consume one non-stacking Receipt for a bounded damage rider; spending the Receipt clears it first.
  • Stop the Pendulum · ActivePaid brief Ward. It is a legal cooling donor or recipient for Borrow Tomorrow; no automatic cooldown refund.
Fancy a different date?

Passive route: Borrow Tomorrow / Clockstrike / Small Hours (passive: exchange Borrow’s fallback penetration for brief Ward). Without two other actives, use the fallback; this trades cooldown-transfer access for a simpler Attack-led route.

Before we commit

New explicit cooldown transfer/Receipt contract and mage tome migration. Native Parry reductions still apply; no negative cooldowns, repeated transfers, refunds or extra actions.

Why I ranked this match here

One of the clearest missing decisions: exchange future availability at a real cost, rather than another stored elemental burst.

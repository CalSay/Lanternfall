A little about me

Nim once stitched banners for a lord who forgot the names beneath them. They now travel with a plain cloak, a bow and a list that grows shorter very slowly.

You’ll recognise me by…

A nonbinary adult with a narrow triangular face, pale skin, freckles and close-cropped chestnut hair. Plum-and-cream tailored coat, unornamented bow and needle-etched lantern.

Combat gear: Bow + quiver. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: bleed pacing and voluntary expiry

Attack earns resource. An authored arrow starts a single finite wound. Reopen it to preserve steady damage, or close it early for a bounded direct release.

It’s complicated:

Renewing the wound prevents its natural expiry reward; expired and consumed are different events. No unlimited stack or tick-trigger recursion.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

A little about me

Maud broke prison chains for a living and remembered the wrists inside them. She hunts the keeper who made her forge the last lock.

You’ll recognise me by…

A tall broad woman in her forties with a long scarred face, warm brown skin and cropped copper hair. Clean charcoal armour, single intact waist panel; fixed poleaxe blade and heavy lantern. No swinging chain weapon.

Combat gear: Two-handed combat poleaxe (future gear). Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: reach restraint without throws

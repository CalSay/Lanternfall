Art review notes

Elderly face and muted blue fit. Shield is more substantial than intended; added hood broadens the silhouette.

A little about me

Gideon guarded caravans for thirty years without losing a passenger. The one empty carriage he brought home still follows him in his dreams.

You’ll recognise me by…

A lean elderly man with a long rectangular face, dark skin and close grey beard. Weather-blue intact coat, small practical shield, plain straight sword and shuttered hip lantern.

Combat gear: Warblade + shield. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: visible-threat interception

Attack earns resource. Prepare one Watch. When a foe visibly gathers, spend Watch on a paid interruption attempt; otherwise choose a reliable penetrating strike or abandon it for protection. No hidden future-move prediction.

It’s complicated:

Preparation buys a choice, not a guaranteed boss skip. A rejected interruption consumes Watch and grants no success reward. Never wait helplessly: ordinary attacks and the damage branch remain useful.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Take Watch · ActivePaid preparation establishes one replaceable Watch; no damage or immediate control.
  • Across the Gate · ActivePaid sword strike; consume Watch for penetration, or, only against a visibly gathering foe, exchange that rider for one charge-disruption attempt through normal core locks. Ordinary damage remains on rejection.
  • Kept Vigil · PassiveAfter wholly manually defending one foe move while Watch exists, bank one Answer once per Watch. Attack may consume Watch and Answer for brief Ward after contact, sacrificing the interrupt setup. No automatic counter.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Take Watch / Across the Gate / Stand Down (consume Watch for Ward and an eligible self-cleanse instead of offence).

Before we commit

Watch/Answer provenance and core interruption contract; existing gear. No Pin, automatic control or extra turns.

Why I ranked this match here

Makes the currently displayed charge the central melee decision rather than a side utility card. Kestrel and Brynja already disrupt charges, so the full kit must prove a stronger identity.

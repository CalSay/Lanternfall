Art review notes

Thickset older fighter and compact matching gauntlets are strong identity matches. Gloves are heavily padded.

A little about me

Rufus carried stone for a monastery that would not let him through its front gate. He left the stones there and took its promise of shelter with him.

You’ll recognise me by…

A thickset older man with a flattened nose, brown skin and shaved head. Plain blue work tunic, wrapped forearms, compact iron gauntlets and broad hip lantern. No giant floating fists.

Combat gear: Combat gauntlets + bracer (future gear). Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: close-range fist fighter

Attack earns resource. Alternate authored lead-hand and rear-hand active strikes to prime a single Finish; spend it on a hard punch or a defensive cover.

It’s complicated:

The passive does not create punches by itself. Every strike takes a normal hero turn; a three-slot loadout trades a defence option for sequence efficiency.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Lead Hand · ActivePaid close strike that primes Lead.
  • Rear Hand · ActivePaid close strike; consumes Lead to prime Finish, otherwise works as a normal blow.
  • Tight Guard · PassivePassive: an Attack may consume Finish for brief Guard after contact rather than using a heavy finisher.
Fancy a different date?

All-active: Lead Hand / Rear Hand / Breakthrough (consume Finish for a heavy direct punch).

Before we commit

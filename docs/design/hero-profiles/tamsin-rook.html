A little about me

Tamsin learned sword forms from the tolling of a cracked watch bell. She still counts the missing note, waiting for the day she can bring its maker home.

You’ll recognise me by…

Tall, strong woman with a square jaw, close black undercut and a burn scar along one cheek. Clean burgundy fencing coat over compact armour; small round shield and warm belt lantern. No bell weapon.

Combat gear: Warblade + shield. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: timing mastery without defence dependence

Attack builds resource. A timed cut earns one Accent only on Perfect. A later cut can spend Accent on damage, or a defensive action can spend it on recovery.

It’s complicated:

Perfect improves a branch rather than being required to function. One Accent maximum; a miss never disables the whole kit.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Downbeat · ActiveTimed sword cut; ordinary success deals useful damage, Perfect banks one Accent.
  • Rest Note · ActivePaid Guard; spend Accent for a modest self-cleanse instead of saving it for the next cut.
  • Measured Hand · PassivePassive: an Attack following an active may spend Accent for penetration. No bonus Attack or extra proc.

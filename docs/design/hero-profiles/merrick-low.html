A little about me

Merrick stopped speaking after his brother’s voice came back from the forest without him. He answers with chalk marks, and never wastes a second arrow on the same warning.

You’ll recognise me by…

A compact middle-aged man with a broad nose, dark brown skin and shaved head. Muted green fitted jacket, ivory chalk tabs, plain bow; hooded lantern with narrow light slit.

Combat gear: Bow + quiver. Every hero carries a personal lantern.

My love language: cadence and low-spend precision

Attack earns resource and advances a capped three-step cadence. Quiet Release snapshots and consumes the opening cadence: second step gives penetration, third gives direct power. Other actives reset cadence before applying their own stated preparation. Manual Parry/Dodge never changes cadence.

It’s complicated:

Spending a hero turn on safety resets your shot rhythm; manual defence does not. Early releases work normally. No free Attack is generated.

Our first fight: three slots

Attack, Parry and Dodge remain available outside these slots. Attack builds the hero’s resource; costs and cooldowns need later tuning.

  • Quiet Release · ActivePaid arrow; consumes current cadence for its declared second-step or third-step payoff.
  • Break Silence · ActivePaid Weaken shot that resets cadence in exchange for immediate safety. This is explicitly tagged as a defensive active for Steady Breath.
  • Steady Breath · PassivePassive: the first Attack after a defensive active starts cadence at one extra step, bounded once per action.

All-active: Quiet Release / Break Silence / Hold Breath (paid preparation: reset the old cadence, then establish one new step). Trade the passive’s defensive-action follow-up for deliberate setup.

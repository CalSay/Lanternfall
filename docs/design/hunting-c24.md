# C24 Hunting: numbers for sign-off

**Status:** mechanics approved by Claude on 30 September 2026 ([sign-off](https://github.com/CalSay/Lanternfall/issues/25#issuecomment-5918464534)); implementation is behind `HUNT_TUNE.on = false`. Public entry additionally requires `huntingArtReady()`, currently false. No art is integrated. Issue #25 replaces the old “Hide is fight-only” rule; Essence and Trophies remain fight-only. The owner clarified that targets are hostile beasts and the tool is a hunting spear. ## Proposed rate and gates

Use the existing gathering clock, Hunting skill, Storehouse Hide grades, level gates and Glint. A beast has a progress/HP bar driven by `S.gProg`. Completing it grants **5 Hide and 5 × the ordinary node XP**; the next hostile beast replaces it. Set its work time to **5 × Fibre's node time** (`CRAFT_NODES.hide.time = 4.5`, since Fibre is 0.9). This keeps Hide units/hour and Hunting XP/hour equal to Fibre at the same skill level and modifiers, while one beast lasts long enough for several strikes. A manual hit keeps the gathering engine's current +0.12 progress. Beast falls emit `harvest`, never combat `kill`: no gold, Essence, Trophies, bestiary credit, parry or combat loot. Existing fight Hide drops remain additive.

The issue's Soft/Tough/Scaled labels describe its three beast grades; the shipped material names are Rawhide/Leather/Wolfhide and need no rename. Grade 4–5 beasts need the later region design. Their gates should stay the existing levels 64/112, with the same formula only after those targets are named.

Profile: Foraging at each gate level 1/14/30; no equipped gear, no Omen or home ground, default 1% tool-mastery speed, no active taps, no Storehouse cap. Fibre measured 1,554/1,506/1,534 units/hour; a same-skill Common Lv 1 Hand has a 10% share and four-hour hauls of 621/602/613. The proposed 5× time/yield/XP keeps those rates if Hunting starts with the same default tool mastery. An existing off-skill Hand would get half: about 310/301/306 per shift. A fresh, uninterrupted grade-1 Fibre-equivalent skill reaches level 14 in about 60 minutes (1,845 completed units in the core); C10 still needs a real day-one Tent 3 affordability run.

Batwing Caves, the Bonefield and Beetle Barrows repeat as Hide-dropping types. Give Hunting its own home test on bat/bones/beetle zones: +25% hero yield, or +50% with three mastery stars, matching `CRAFT_HOME_BONUS`. Keep those zones' existing Crystal/Herb/Fibre home bonuses. The base grade-1 hero target becomes about 1,942 or 2,331 Hide/hour there; Hands currently do not receive `homeBonus` in `handsRate`, so do not silently add it to their shifts.

An optional **Tracker** trait is +20% Hide only, additive with direct-yield traits and snapshotted at send. Its same-skill Common Lv 1 four-hour targets are about 745/722/736 Hide. Tracker plus Early Riser reaches the C8 whole-roll limit of 1.40×; Tracker with a fully overlapping Friendly partner gives 1.32×. A swapped single trait is at most +20%, within C8's approved daily margin. Existing queued shift rates stay frozen. Hunter joins the future applicant pool when mechanics are enabled, as approved; existing Hands can hunt off-skill at the current 50% rate.

## Spear and implementation boundaries

The approved Workbench Hunting Spear uses **the Sickle's existing recipe and numeric progression**: grade-1 input 4 Ore + 3 Wood, scaled by `craftScale`; speed 0.6 × item power, double yield 0.1 × power (60% cap), rare find 0.012 × power (8% cap), and the usual +25% right-tier tool speed. This is the approved parity starting price; C10 owns later tuning. Do not alias Hunting to Sickle gear. The spear recipe/stats and 5-Hide bundle have now received balance approval. Visuals can then use a `tool: 'spear'` data hook and a matching vetted asset pack; no ad hoc prop or beast drawing is part of this proposal.

Issue #25 owns `55-gathering.js`, `72-ui-gather.js`, `21-data-craft.js` (balance sign-off), Hands data/engine/UI and a C24 check section. Approved narrow shared extensions: `30-state.js` defaults for `skills.hunt`, `equip.spear`; `55-nav.js` Hunting family/verb/last-node entries; `40-rules.js` spear stat mapping plus the per-node five-unit yield/XP scalar; `50-sim.js` live `harvest()` to award that scalar (its offline gather branch is already generic); and `55-tools.js` spear mastery/defaults. Claude also approved the minimal hidden-entry predicates in crafting/items/UI, setNode, onboarding and goals, plus the exact default assertion updates ([guard approval](https://github.com/CalSay/Lanternfall/issues/25#issuecomment-5919353319)). Stage/hero art integration remains a later gate. Old v5 saves should receive only missing defaults, with no key bump. Checks should pin live/offline Hide parity, 4 h and queued Hands payouts, home grounds, caps, old-save loading, and absence of combat rewards. ## Hidden mechanics integration

The public art gate is deliberately independent of the mechanics flag: setting `HUNT_TUNE.on = true` in a headless check permits Hunting rates and Hands jobs, while `huntingVisible()` remains false. The flag is runtime tuning, not a saved preference. Existing v5 progression and inventory retain their values; missing Hunting skill, spear equipment and spear mastery receive defaults. An imported unreleased Hide selection is reset before first-frame or away processing to normal fighting, ore tier 1 and zero gathering progress; its Hunting XP and spear records remain intact. `nav.last.hunt` is filled lazily.

`huntingRenderData(t)` names the three beast keys (`enraged-boar`, `bristleback-wolf`, `fen-lizard`) and `tool: 'spear'`. Scene, sprite and effects are null. The future beast HP display uses the gathering work fraction, `1 - S.gProg`; a `harvest` event marks a fall and replacement. The live resolver awards the five-unit scalar once; the generic away and Hands formulas receive it through `nodeYieldAvg`. Existing fight Hide and non-Hunting home families retain their behavior. Grades 4–5 have no beast and cannot be selected or assigned to Hands.

The complete owner-approved pack, its stage/hero adapter, Hunting view and dedicated icons must land together before public readiness changes. No existing gathering scene, hero pose, beast sprite or tool icon is a Hunting substitute. Review drafts are separate from the mechanics commit and do not count as approval.

# C22 Sunken Coast: final-count encounter proposal

Design proposal for owner and Claude review, 1 October 2026. This applies the owner-approved [world structure](world-structure.md): seven existing Coast areas, five different zones per area, one unique monster for each zone, five active regular fights against that monster followed by its named **Shadowborn Captain**, then the area's **Champion of Darkness**. After the seventh Champion, Silas Penrow is the separate regional **Elder of Darkness**. Thus the Coast proposes **35 zone monsters, 35 same-design Captain enhancements, seven Champions and Silas: 43 unique enemy designs and 218 one-enemy fights**. Zones run 36–70 once; there are no area I–V repeats, gauntlets, queued adds, simultaneous actors, automated next fights, Auto combat or offline combat rewards. Bosses are fought once; any return would need a separately justified stronger encounter and is not designed here. The count and sequence are owner decisions; names, silhouettes, moves and relative balance below remain review proposals. No runtime or art changes are included.

The zone creatures emerged with the darkness as distinct agents. They are not corrupted ordinary wildlife, drowned townspeople or household objects with eyes. Hunting alone uses corrupted wildlife. “Shroud” means the established darkness, not a new boss. Silas alone keeps his human history, Oath lens and existing Great Lantern story role. Each creature fights from a reachable shore, wreck deck, ledge or nave floor; fliers visibly return to a contact point. Area ecology and tide provide scenery and timing cues, never a hidden second attacker.

## Shared combat and reward notation

Profiles give **ordinary uncritical Attack actions to kill** with suitable zone gear, relative Speed to a hero at 1.0, physical armour 0/1/2 (provisional 0%/10%/20% physical reduction), and one weakness/resistance from physical, holy, poison, fire, frost; all omitted types are neutral. Normal target toughness is 3–5 actions. A Captain inherits its monster's anatomy, two moves, type, weakness and resistance, but has 6–8-action toughness (6 when the normal is 3, 7 when 4, 8 when 5), +0.1 Speed capped at 1.55, a readable palette recolour plus a fixed Captain outline mark from the same art pack, and the named third move below. Captain armour stays the same. This enhancement is one enemy, not another unique design or a second actor; its third move is part of the shared monster-and-Captain pose inventory. If that move needs a distinct anticipation, contact, hold or release key, plan it in the same design pack up front; the Captain remains the same creature design, not another unique monster. Normal foes can take at most two consecutive opportunities; Champions and Silas at most three.

Each listed damage percentage is **per landed hit** against a reference hero's maximum HP before defence/mitigation, with the multi-hit total in parentheses. It is a calibration unit converted to fixed zone damage in implementation, never a live percent-of-player-HP attack. Normal move totals mostly sit at 20–35%; a five-hit move does not mean five full-strength attacks. Every contact has a visible cue and permits parry or dodge. Each parry refunds one from every active ability cooldown; an all-parried complete move earns one counter. Feints, wind-ups and charge starts do no damage. A rider applies only on its specified landed hit and only once per move. Proposed common incoming hero debuffs: Bleed 1 = 2% reference HP at each of the next two hero starts; Burn 1 = 4% at each of the next two; Venom 1 = 2% at each of the next two, pending runtime approval; Chill 1 = 10% Speed reduction for two hero opportunities (max two stacks); Weaken 1 = 25% lower direct output for the next hero opportunity; Blind 1 = 30% miss chance on the next direct action, never on a reaction. Reapply the stronger effect and refresh its timer. Coast currently assigns no Venom rider. When estimating missed-move lethality, add the stated landed rider to direct damage: Bleed adds up to 4%, Burn up to 8%, and Chill/Weaken/Blind change subsequent opportunities rather than the printed hit total. Three missed 20–35% normal moves therefore span roughly 60–105% before these riders and recovery; that is a calibration warning, not a survival guarantee.

A normal has two distinct moves. The move names, hit counts, damage types, timing and anatomical cues below are the complete basic script; alternate them until both have been seen. A Captain alternates the two inherited moves with its third after first exposure, without surprise consecutive third moves. Seven body pose keys (idle, two wind-ups, two contacts, hurt, unravel) are a starting estimate for a simple normal, not a cap. Inventory the complete two-move normal and three-move Captain kit together, including every anticipation, contact, hold, release, projectile and reduced-motion cue needed for readable timing. Reuse a pose only when the anatomy and hit cue still read correctly; additional Captain attack poses can be authored within the same creature design pack. There are no procedural limb systems, invisible damage windows, combat material drops or newly invented resources.

Regulars and Captains award Gold and Essence, plus relics only where existing eligibility allows. Champions retain the former Coast elder **Trophy** route proposed for role remapping, Gold and Essence, and the existing eligible relic and occasional unique opportunities. Existing unique identities appear beside each Champion below, but whether/how a one-time boss awards them is **open**, as [world structure](world-structure.md) records; no guaranteed first-kill or invented rematch roll is implied. Silas keeps his existing nonmaterial Great Lantern unlock, with unique/relic policy likewise open. No foe drops wood, fibre, herbs, gems, ore, hides or Tide Sigils. Hunting/gathering remain separate.

## Grey Shingle · zones 36–40

## Champion and Elder charge rules

Each Champion's charge begins by consuming one foe opportunity with **no damage**. Its named release may occur only on a later foe opportunity after the hero has received at least one actual opportunity, regardless of Speed. The hero can interrupt during that gap through a non-locked Stun/Freeze contribution or at least 6% of the Champion's actual maximum HP in damage dealt after charge start; DoT HP damage counts, Ward damage does not. An interruption cancels the release and spends the next foe opportunity on harmless recovery; one ordinary move must resolve before another charge, and control skip plus recovery never stacks into two free losses. On release, every listed hit has its own defence cue and reaction choice. Phase changes never erase the hero response. These are the [C22 final contract](enemies-c22-final-contract.md) technical rules; exact gauge and frame timing require implementation testing. The Champions are area bosses fought once, not elders relabelled as repeatable zone foes.

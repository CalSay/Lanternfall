Combat screen: draft for the owner (UX-C1)

An interactive mock-up of the solo combat screen. It is a design draft, not game code: numbers, foe art and the boss are placeholders. Tap the slots in the phone to try them. Keys: Q W E for abilities, A S D for Parry, Dodge and Attack (Space also dodges). Long-press an ability slot, or tap an empty one, to choose what goes in it.

Icons and frames

24×24 pixel icons drawn in code, shown at 2× (as on the bar) and 1×. Ability slots use the gold frame; Parry, Dodge and Attack use the plain iron frame. A Hallowed ability gets the white-gold frame, a halo mark above the slot, and its own colours.

Notes

Layout proposal

  • Landscape phone (the main target). The tabs move to a left rail (52 px) with the portrait and level on top. The left thumb navigates; the right thumb fights. The bottom edge stays free, so the stage gets the full height. One 44 px top row holds everything not combat-related: gold, embers, Fight/Mining, Switch, the zone arrows and the bell. The stage is 480×316 at 1 art px = 1 CSS px: the 480×270 art sits at the bottom and the top 46 px is night sky that carries the combat info (hero HP and statuses on the left; foe or boss name, HP, statuses and the boss timer on the right). The right column (208 px) is the thumb zone: Next Up and notices at the top, the action bar in the bottom-right corner.
  • Portrait phone (interim). The owner's order: header 48, control row (Fight/Mining, Switch, zone arrows), Next Up, the stage (360×369, art cropped to the middle 360 columns at 1:1), the action bar, the 54 px tabs. Notices sit at the bottom of the stage.
  • Desktop. The landscape layout at 2×: rail 64, top row 48, stage 960×672 (the art at exactly 2×), right column 256 with Next Up, notices and the bar. Key labels show on the slots.
  • Why a left rail and not a slim bottom bar in landscape: height is the scarce thing at 360 px. A 36 px bottom bar would take the stage from 316 to 280 px and put tabs right under the action bar, where a mis-tap in a boss fight opens a menu. The rail costs 52 px of width, which the 740 px screen can spare (480 stage + 208 bar column).
  • Taps: Parry, Dodge and Attack fire on touch-down (timing matters). Ability slots fire on release, so a long-press can open the picker without casting. An empty slot opens the picker on a tap.
  • Heavy hits: during a boss wind-up, Parry and Dodge get a red pulsing edge and a "!" badge, the stage shows a "Heavy hit" chip and a red patch under the hero. In the last 0.35 s (the parry window) Parry turns steady gold and the chip says "Parry now". Dodge works in the last 0.9 s.

Questions for the owner

  1. Choosing abilities: is long-press on a slot (plus tap on an empty slot) the right way in, or should changing slots live only in the Hero menu, so a slot tap always casts?
  2. Landscape tabs: left rail (shown) or a slim bottom bar? And when a tab opens a menu in landscape: over the stage with the bar hidden (the hero keeps fighting on auto), or a split screen with a smaller stage?
  3. Next Up in landscape: in the right column above the bar (shown), or in the top row next to the zone arrows?
  4. Attack size: keep all six slots the same size (shown), or make Attack a bit bigger, as the most-used button?
  5. Hold to attack: while you are playing actively, does holding Attack repeat, or is it one tap per swing? Does auto-attack stay on while you play?
  6. The parry hint: is the gold "Parry now" glow in the window too much help? It could be a setting, or only shown for the first few bosses.
  7. Melee reach: Tobin lunges to the front foe and back (shown). Keep that, or does he stand and fight at range with shockwaves?
  8. Notches: on phones with a notch on the right in landscape, the safe area pushes the bar in by up to about 44 px. OK to keep the bar at the safe-area edge (the side column gets narrower)?
  9. Hero HP colour: green for the hero, red for foes (shown). XP is also green today; fine, or should hero HP be another colour?

## Counts and boundaries

34 total: **11 Warriors, 8 Rangers, 15 Mages**. Only Wren, Tobin and Pip are implemented starters; all expanded kits and other heroes remain design/progression work.

Owner art corrections still apply, including the selected bow designs, Peregrine’s hair, Flint’s proportions, and Ione/Adela staff/tome details. Preserve Sable’s liked design. Whole-pack approval and pose lock are still required for production poses. Every hero carries a lantern; Mage staff+tome compatibility still requires the documented gear migration.

Rufus stays withdrawn under the no-unarmed rule. Hilda, Roscoe, Petra and Nim remain outside the selected roster; Petra’s kit-review requirement remains. Ludo and Maud stay deferred for crafting. Existing NPC, benched and retired directions are unchanged.

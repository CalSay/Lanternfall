## Direction

The first draft overextended the earlier all-32-playable proposal. Making every occupation a fighter
produced many convincing NPCs, unclear weapons and repeated ornate travel costumes. Revise the roster
around distinct combat decisions, clear equipment and readable game-scale designs. Preserve the characters,
stories and useful ideas; assigning an NPC role does not delete their identity or immediately move live content.

Working recommendation: **20 playable candidates**, **8 NPC candidates**, **3 held for major rethink**,
and **Anselm's playable concept retired**. This is a design shortlist, not a promise to ship 20 complete packs.
No replacement for Anselm is needed until the remaining roster's missing playstyle is established.

Playable candidates: Wren, Tobin, Pip, Hesketh, Bram, Maren, Aldric, Kestrel, Thessaly, Grenna,
Isolde, Oriel, Elowen, Caedmon, Corvin, Cass, Linnet, Eskil, Inga, Ragna.
NPC candidates: Morwen, Vesper, Loveday, Davy, Ferrin, Hob, Beatrix, Solveig.
Major-rethink bench: Oswin, Brynja, Asta.

## Equipment and skills/crafting dependency

Current inspected gear: Warblade/Shield for Warden, Bow/Quiver for Ranger, Staff/Lantern for
Lanternmage, Censer/Tome for Lightkeeper. Gathering Woodaxe and Hunting Spear are tools with
gathering stats, not combat weapon categories. There is no Grimoire kind in this table.
Historical companion comments do not establish new solo hero equipment permissions.

Create a future **skills/crafting overhaul** work item covering:

- Combat axe, greatsword/duelling blade, combat spear/harpoon, hammer/maul, dagger and eligible classes.
- Staff + tome as Cal's mage visual/equipment direction; define compatibility, recipes and stats explicitly
  instead of silently equipping the current Lightkeeper Tome on every mage.
- Two-handed rule: recommended weapon occupies both hands and excludes handheld off-hand equipment;
  an attached focus/insignia may supply an appropriate build choice only through a deliberately designed
  accessory rule. It must replace/rebalance the current off-hand contribution, never add a ninth free slot.
  Confirm handling for bows/quivers and casting staffs/tomes too; carried or belt-stowed equipment is valid.
- Names/icons/inventory filters that distinguish Combat Spear from Hunting Spear and combat axe from Woodaxe.
- Stat/affix pools, recipes, stations, unlocks, salvage/upgrades, equipped save compatibility and any migrations.
- Preserve invested items and current progression; no automatic conversion or deletion in this planning task.

Do not freeze a weapon-dependent production master until its hand use and legal equipment layout are settled.
A hero does not need two separate handheld props just because the current UI has an off-hand slot.

## Work sequence and acceptance

1. **Write a player-readable roster/kit dossier first.** One page per candidate: class/subclass, magic/weapon,
   main and secondary/stowed equipment, combat decision, resource, strengths/trade-offs and 3 example builds.
   Use three selectable actives/passives; ordinary Attack and manual defence remain available. Label every
   kit as proposed versus implemented. Include Stars and explain what each adds beyond raw damage.
   Resolve Eskil/Wren, Oswin/Tobin/Maren and the mage overlaps before commissioning more pictures.
2. **Produce the equipment/crafting design note.** Separate immediate supported gear from future categories;
   settle two-handed and mage tome rules and record the future overhaul dependency. No crafting code yet.
3. **Revise one motion-free pilot group.** Wren (bow/closed eyes), Tobin (face/hair/headwear), Oriel
   (face/recognisable tome), and Linnet (distinct face/frost-glass focus/tome) test geometry and identity
   corrections. Only after the dossier/equipment review; no automatic generation in this planning task.
4. **Review at native game size before expanding.** Full-size boards alone hid problems. Show 224×192
   native masters and game-scale previews together; require recognisable face/silhouette/equipment, both
   arms, connected limbs, matched boots, contained weapons, correct bow geometry and few intact cloth masses.
   Build a face comparison sheet to vary age, jaw, nose, eye/brow shape, complexion and expression—not hair only.
5. **Complete bounded concept revisions by role.** Accepted hero directions first, NPC sheets separately,
   bench characters only after their role earns a clear niche. Correct close-up/full-figure prop agreement,
   including Bram's rotated engraving. Keep drafts for owner review; stop after one failed bounded attempt.
6. **Lock approved masters, then animation.** Use mandatory pose-lock + generate2dsprite. Do not build kits
   from these unapproved boards. Fixed source identity, equipment reference, palette and anchors; body/FX
   separated. The Pip experiment shows gates can accept an erased hand: visual anatomy review is a required
   gate alongside command results, never substituted by a mechanical pass. No approved-pack/runtime changes.
7. **Validate replayability on the reduced shortlist.** Designer/balance reviewer compare three-slot routes
   with and without Stars against the same encounter scenarios. Require genuinely different decisions,
   non-mandatory signatures, costs/risks, viable passive-only routes where offered and no proc/refund loops.
   Reuse insights from parked kits only where they strengthen a remaining hero; do not copy entire kits as skins.

First reviewable milestone: **the revised hero/NPC shortlist plus hero-focused kit/equipment dossier**.
It answers what the characters actually do before another expensive art batch. No game roster migration,
new weapon, shop, crafting feature, art approval or deployment is performed by this plan.

Read-only specialist review confirmed the disposition accounts for all 32 characters and found strong
proposed niches for Hesketh, Eskil, Linnet and Ragna; Oswin remains the weakest distinction. Ragna/Cass
poison overlap also needs direct comparison. Equipment findings were checked against the craft table.
This is design review, not measured balance approval. Planning-only change: no new gameplay tests run.

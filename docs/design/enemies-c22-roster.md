# Lanternfall: full enemy roster for pose planning

Owner-approved structure: **175 zone monsters +35 Champions of Darkness +5 Elders of Darkness =215 unique enemies**. Each zone monster also has its own named Shadowborn Captain enhancement, detailed in its regional design file. Captains share the monster design but may need extra attack poses in the same pack; they do not add unique species. Names and moves are review proposals, not generated art or implemented enemies.

Read the [final contract](enemies-c22-final-contract.md) for fight counts, rewards, status rules and open balance decisions. Monster anatomy and attacks determine the required poses; existing frames and preliminary key estimates do not limit the design.

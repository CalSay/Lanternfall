# C22 fantasy direction: concept review, not final roster

> **Structure now finalized:** [Final roster contract](enemies-c22-final-contract.md) records175 zone monsters,35 Champions and5 Elders (215 unique), plus175 Captain enhancements. This earlier exploration is retained as design history, not the current count or role contract.

Owner direction, 1 October 2026. **The owner is discussing the target count with Claude. Final roster selection, enemy count, variants and art production remain open.** These documents explore and rank concepts; their number of rows is not an approved production count or quota per area.

## What changed

Combat foes are agents that emerged with the darkness: fiends, impossible predators, sorcerous soldiers and purpose-built horrors. They need exciting silhouettes and moves justified by their anatomy. A new name on an ordinary animal is insufficient. Defeat can release stolen light as the dark body unravels; it does not routinely restore a rabbit or a villager.

Hunting is separate: existing animals driven into a rage by the dark. The three Hunting combat cards in the earlier systems draft are withdrawn. The approved Hunting art and gathering loop stay in their existing roles.

One enemy per fight, with no gauntlets, queues, adds or forced follow-on battle. Repeated encounters with a memorable foe are acceptable. All combat requires active input. Gold, Essence and existing eligible relic rolls stay; some bosses can award uniques and elders retain their existing Trophies. No enemy material drops.

Established story bosses remain deliberate exceptions: the Fenmother, Silas, Ser Durand, Whitehush and the Voice are not casually replaced or given new origins. The latest owner decisions relayed by Claude settle two points: Durand is the region boss only, with a separate last-area boss; the Voice is one large fight with phases, not five appearances. Bosses never repeat as the same encounter; a justified return must be a new, stronger encounter. The distribution of bosses across area passes, and therefore the boss count, remain undecided. Do not assume seven/six elder concepts fill every pass or add repeat farming.

Elder silhouettes are also candidates, not new approved boss slots. Do not generate a variant for every pitch by default.

## Selection criteria

1. Would the silhouette be recognisable without a nameplate or recolour?
2. Does it feel like an agent of the darkness with intent, rather than ordinary wildlife or a household prop?
3. Does the body explain a memorable timing lesson: unfolding blades, a contracting light cage, a held jaw or an opening core?
4. Can the player parry or dodge every actual hit and understand the tell on a small landscape screen?
5. Can it be animated with a compact authored sprite pack, fixed contact alignment and bounded effects?
6. Does it add something another candidate does not already do? Shared rigs are useful, duplicate experiences are not.

The first selected Mossy Hollow foe still targets roughly three ordinary uncritical hits. Later relative combat numbers are provisional, not tested balance. Alternative drafts currently use different provisional incoming status magnitudes; these are comparison notes, not multiple final status implementations. Resolve one incoming Bleed/Burn/Venom contract before final cards; enemy Venom needs explicit approval even though hero Venom abilities were deferred. Seven normal pose keys is a default estimate, not a hard cap: named eight-key proposals require cost review before art. Core Speed, control and reaction rules remain from the C19 handoff. No new combat systems are authorized by a silhouette pitch.

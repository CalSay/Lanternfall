# C22 — Emberwaste encounter roster proposal

> **Story note (2026-10-05, story judge ruling b):** each Shadowborn emerges whole from the dark in a shape copied from the place or from memory, and is never the thing it copies (`story-bible.md` rule 4). A story line may claim a copied likeness only where the sprite shows it.
> **Story note (ruling a):** the Emberwaste Elder is the Pyre Knight, the Lea's held fire wearing Ser Durand's shape. Its shield moves and spoken cues are the fire's, in Durand's voice (lent); the real Durand is held voiceless inside the Pyre and carried out alive by Caedmon after the fight (`story-bible.md` 5.2, 8.3). Moves and numbers below are unchanged; only the subject is renamed when built.

**Status:** complete design proposal for review; no runtime, artwork, loot-table, or progression changes. This roster follows the owner's confirmed world shape in [world-structure.md](world-structure.md): Emberwaste has 35 zone monsters, seven area Champions of Darkness, and Ser Durand as the region's Elder of Darkness. A Shadowborn Captain is a named enhancement of the zone's monster, not a new species or art design. Each area is played once; each zone has five separate regular fights against its own monster followed by one Captain fight. One foe appears in each fight. The player chooses when to enter every fight; no queued fights, automatic transitions, repeat bosses, or farming loops.

The ordinary roster consists only of beings formed from the Voice's darkness and Emberwaste's elemental conditions. None is corrupted wildlife, an escaped villager's ghost, an animated household object, or a captured lamp. Ser Durand's established story is preserved. He appears once as the region boss after the seven areas; his fight mechanics and phase script below remain proposals for review, not approved implementation values. The Pyre has its own Champion, separate from Durand.

## Shared card rules

- Values are fixed zone-calibration targets against a reference hero, not percentages that scale with player HP upgrades. Normal toughness is 3–5 ordinary, uncritical Attack actions; Captain toughness is 6–8; area Champions and the region Elder target 10–15. Each card gives speed, physical armour, and one weakness and resistance. Armour is light 10%, medium 20%, heavy 30%; weaknesses use ×1.5, resistances ×0.6; unlisted types are neutral. Damage types are physical, holy, poison, fire, and frost, exactly one per hit.
- Every move states its tell, number of contacts, damage per hit, and total. Each actual hit offers its own parry or dodge. Normal single hits are 20–35% of reference maximum HP; multi-hit moves split comparable totals. A status needs a landed hit and applies once on the named hit: Bleed deals 2% HP at each of the next two hero starts; Burn deals 4% HP at each of the next two hero starts; Venom deals 2% HP at each of the next two hero starts but remains pending runtime approval and is not assigned to any card here; Chill slows hero speed by 10% per stack for two hero opportunities, maximum two stacks; Weaken reduces direct output by 25% for one hero opportunity; Blind gives a 30% chance the next direct action misses and never affects defence or hides cues. Ward absorption alone does not prevent a status; a parry or dodge does, unless a card explicitly requires HP damage. Refresh the strongest existing instance; do not add hidden stacks.
- Damage uses only the existing identifiers physical, holy, poison, fire, and frost. Frost is not a new damage type; this proposal adds no damage types.
- The shared scheduler caps ordinary foes at two consecutive opportunities; Champions and the region Elder may take at most three. Multi-hit actions are one opportunity. Every Champion and Durand has a charge that deals no damage while winding up, gives at least one hero opportunity, and releases later. Interrupt before release with accepted control or actual HP damage equal to 6% of boss maximum HP. Ward damage does not count; DoT HP damage does. An interrupted charge consumes the move and grants one recovery, with no second control skip.
- Normal enemies and Captains award Gold and area-grade Essence; preserve existing relic eligibility and rates, without making every foe relic-eligible. Area Champions retain existing Trophy rewards and Gold/Essence; do not add new Trophy families. The Elder keeps existing Trophy/relic eligibility. The policy for any unique boss item remains open; no repeat boss farming is allowed. No enemy drops ore, wood, fibre, hide, herbs, gems, or other gathering materials.
- Art counts are estimates, not caps. A normal's seven-key estimate is idle, two wind-ups, two contact keys, hurt, and defeat. A Shadowborn Captain shares its base monster's anatomy and design pack, with its named recolour and third move; plan any extra attack poses needed for a clear tell/contact in that same pack. Champions and Durand need larger, distinct physical silhouettes; seven keys is only an estimate, and the art pass should add poses wherever four-move readability needs them. New art is permitted for all roster entries; these notes describe scope, not a restriction on the attack design.

## Zone roster: 35 monsters and their Shadowborn Captains

Every area below has five zone-specific species, played in zone order. For each zone, the five ordinary fights use that monster; a separate sixth fight uses its named Captain. The Captain inherits both base moves and rewards, receives the listed 6–8-action toughness and one extra move, and reuses the base art.

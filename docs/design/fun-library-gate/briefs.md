# Idea briefs from the fun library (input to the idea gate)
Written 2026-10-05 by the f-fun-library thread. Evidence = `research/fun-library.md` principle (P) or quit theme (Q), counts are floors.
Repo rules to respect: no prestige or resets; combat is active only (no Auto, no away combat earnings), gathering stays idle;
nothing pay-to-win; no animation (icons and still images); one-screen mobile layout at 360px; save compatibility is sacred;
online layer is off limits. Coverage areas are rows of `autopilot/coverage-map.md`.

| id | Idea | Player problem and evidence | Pillar / area | Predicted effect | Cost | Risk | Switch-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Away report is the front door**: one card on open listing gathered goods, a single Collect button and the next goal | Players in 6+ games praise coming back to progress (P1, 130). Our away report exists but its clarity is unmeasured | calm gathering; area 13, 2 | Seconds from open to first action under 10; taps to collect = 1 | small UI | Could crowd the one-screen layout | Hide card behind a setting |

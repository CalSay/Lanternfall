# C22 fantasy exploration — the Pale Reach

**Working candidates, not an approved roster or count.** The owner has requested a full fantasy rewrite: combat enemies are agents born or newly emerged with the darkness, not corrupted wildlife, the remains of villagers, or possessed household objects. This explicitly supersedes the previous general origin rule. Corrupted existing wildlife belongs to Hunting, which this document does not turn into combat. The owner is discussing roster size with Claude; the twelve sketches below explore two directions for each area and do not select twelve for implementation.

Only this exploration document changes. The original `enemies-c22-pale-reach.md` remains a historical design draft, not the source of approved current origins. Whitehush retains its established story as an explicit exception; this exploration makes no decision on its move count or phases.

## Shared assumptions for these sketches

One hero fights **one enemy per fight**. There are no queues, gauntlets, summoned combat actors or unattended next fights. Active player choices drive combat. Pause freezes the encounter; absence grants no combat damage, kills, rewards or progression. Every actual contact permits parry or dodge. Each successful parry refunds one from all active cooldowns; only an entirely parried move produces one counter. Feints are harmless preparation, never concealed hits. Speed cannot shorten reaction windows.

Provisional reference figures support comparison only: Speed 1.0 alternates; above1 can occasionally double, below1 sometimes allows the hero to double; normal cap two consecutive opportunities. toughness 3–5 means neutral, uncritical ordinary Attack actions, with normal reference armour reflected, not a guaranteed count for finishers or a resisted kit. Damage percentages are fixed-zone calibration targets against reference HP, not damage that increases when the player buys more HP. Armour affects physical damage; weakness/resistance use only phys, holy, poison, fire, frost, with all unlisted types neutral. Numerical tuning and actual animation seconds remain open.

A listed Chill is one stack,10% reference Speed reduction for two subsequent hero opportunities, maximum two stacks under the shared contract. Weaken is25% less direct output for one hero opportunity. A Bleed rider is2% reference HP on each of two subsequent hero starts, refresh strongest, no hidden stacks. Riders apply once, only on the identified contact landing through defence. A move listing no rider applies none. Do not add reflected damage, ambient cold or an automatic freeze.

Rewards: gold and Essence; relics only under existing eligibility, never a new universal relic roll. Bosses may have occasional approved uniques. Elders retain **Trophies** and existing first-clear/progression flags. No ore, wood, hide, herbs, fibre, gems, Sigils or disguised material caches are enemy rewards. No new currency is proposed. All art notes are future pose requirements; no art generation or runtime change is included.

## Frostgate Pass — exploration candidates

### Frostmaw

**Concept and anatomy:** a four-armed predator emerged from the first dark seam in the pass. Its head is a vertical split between two long ivory jaw plates; there are no eyes, ears, fur or recognisable wolf skull. Two heavy forearms carry its body, two smaller hooked arms pull food toward the split. It hunts light as movement, rather than being an animal the dark infected.

**Profile:** Speed 1.2, occasional doubles, cap 2; toughness 3 actions; armour 0%; weak poison, no resistance. Poison weakness concerns the strange living membrane within its plates, not a claim that this is wildlife.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Visible tell and rider |
|---|---|---|
| Split Bite | 12%+16%=28% phys, two hits, quick then held | The left jaw plate folds outward before the first bite; the right remains raised for a full hold before its own contact. No rider. |
| Hookfall | 29% phys, one slow hit | Both small arms latch above the mouth; the body rises and the large forearms visibly straighten before falling. No collision damage during the rise. |

**Test/answers:** follow independent jaw plates instead of defending once for a double bite. Wren uses Pinned to widen both contacts; Tobin gains Grit from individual parries and can Bash; Pip uses Frost Shard to restrain its rate. No mandatory hero or damage type. **Buildability:** seven keys—low idle, jaw spread/contact, raised-body hold/drop, hurt, body folding into colourless frost. Separate plate layers keep each bite legible; no restored wolf is drawn. **Rewards:** shared normal reward rules.

### Rime Colossus

**Concept and anatomy:** a heavy six-legged horror condensed when darkness packed the pass's air into solid rime. It has no torso/head division: a horizontal chest-mouth runs between two thick, forward-curved forelimbs, while four short rear limbs support its mass. Its shell is grown matter, not cairns, armour or stones possessing an animal.

**Profile:** Speed 0.75, no natural double; toughness 5; armour 20%; weak poison, no resistance.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |
|---|---|---|
| Jaw Press | 34% phys, one slow hit | The chest-mouth opens sideways; both forelimbs settle flat, then the entire front edge compresses toward the hero. No rider. |
| Underclaw | 12%+16%=28% phys, two hits after a feint | A harmless chest contraction precedes two small underside claws extending separately. Only each bright claw tip carries a contact ring. No rider. |

**Test/answers:** distinguish the immense body preparation from the actual underside claws. Wren's Bleed bypasses armour; Tobin's Sunder answers it; Pip's spells avoid physical armour. **Buildability:** seven keys—low six-legged idle, open-mouth hold/press, underside-claw show/extension, hurt, shell losing its inner tension. No loose rocks or invisible trampling damage. **Rewards:** shared normal rules.

## The Eyries — exploration candidates

### Gale Seraph

**Concept and anatomy:** an angular horror born where the first Whiteout caught the darkness between three cliff faces. Six translucent blade-wings radiate from a long vertical throat, with no bird head, beak or feathers. Two lower wings act as legs; the four upper wings fold into changing wedges. The name describes its unsettling silhouette, not a holy being or a fallen person.

**Profile:** Speed 1.2, occasional doubles; toughness 3; armour 0%; weak poison, no resistance. It stays at weapon-contact height and is always targetable.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |
|---|---|---|
| Wing Verdict | 27% phys, one compact hit | Upper wings form a pointed arch, then one leading edge turns dark against the sky before cutting down. No rider. |
| Sleet Blades | 13%+15%=28% frost, slow then quick | Two upper wings bow separately and each sheds one broad solid blade; decorative sleet cannot hit. No rider. |

**Test/answers:** identify two broad blades rather than a cloud of particles. Wren uses Pinned; Tobin can Brace or Bash without reaching a flying target; Pip's Frost Shard slows its extra opportunities. **Buildability:** seven body keys—radial idle, arch hold/cut, bowed-wing hold/release, hurt, membrane collapse—with two authored projectile silhouettes. It never needs flight-path targeting. **Rewards:** shared normal rules.

### Rift Skimmer

**Concept and anatomy:** a flat triangular creature slid from a dark fault in the wind. Three trailing spinal ribbons end in hooked bone fins; a mouth opens along its underside instead of a face. It rests on those fins while fighting. It is neither a ray carried up the mountain nor a bird coated in frost.

**Profile:** Speed 0.85, slow; toughness 5; armour 15%; weak poison, no resistance.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |
|---|---|---|
| Broadside | 35% phys, one heavy hit | The triangular body turns edge-on, pauses, then opens flat toward the hero. The rotation itself is harmless. |
| Spine and Maw | 12%+18%=30% phys, quick then held | One trailing fin hooks forward; the underside mouth remains visibly open before the separate second contact. No rider. |

**Test/answers:** see a broadside as one impact, not a wing plus hidden body collision. Wren's Bleed, Tobin's Sunder and Pip's neutral spells provide ordinary armour answers. **Buildability:** seven keys—fin-supported idle, edge-on hold/broadside, hooked-fin hold/underside bite, hurt, flat collapsed membrane. Full body remains in melee reach. **Rewards:** shared normal rules.

It closes around fallen light as it moves.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |

It bends by sliding chambers, not slithering like an ordinary snake. The dark is its origin, while ice is the matter of its new body.

**Profile:** Speed 1.2, occasional doubles; toughness 3; armour 0%; weak holy, resists phys.

| Move | Damage and rhythm | Tell and rider |

## Whitehush and remaining decisions

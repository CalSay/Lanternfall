Wren Hollowmere — the Batwing Archer

Revised design candidate; expanded signatures remain proposals

Listening precision archer

Who you play

Eyes closed in combat; violet bat hood and ornate bow. Listen, mark and decide when precision is worth consuming the setup.

Your combat choice

Holding a Mark supports resource income; spending it changes the payoff. Wounds offer a separate route.

Equipment

Bow + quiver (supported categories)

Also carries a lantern. Carriage adds no automatic combat stats or free equipment slot.

Resource

Aim 0–3

Start at zero; ordinary chosen Attack gains one native resource after contact. Cap is the displayed reserve. Named signatures/passives may add only their explicit income, under cap; a qualifying reward is spent even at cap. All reserves, counters and setup reset each fight. This is the revised proposal, not a claim of live new-hero support.

Position and timing rules

Held offensive preparations and next-Attack/signature charges default to two subsequent H and one replacement-only slot per named kind; one charge cannot attach and also be cancelled/consumed for another payout. Native foe statuses retain core F expiry.

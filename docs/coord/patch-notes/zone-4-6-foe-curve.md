Zones 4 to 6 now carry on from zone 3 instead of stepping back. A foe there has a set amount of HP for its zone, so every level and every new weapon makes your fights shorter (before, it always took at least 8 of your Attacks, however strong you were). Zone 4, 5 and 6 foes pay 72 to 74 gold instead of 41 to 45, and their bosses pay 383 to 435 instead of 219 to 264. A zone 4 to 6 foe now hits Wren or Pip for about a fifth of their health when they first arrive, so Parry and Dodge still matter. Tobin's armour still takes most of it. The zone 4 boss, the Elder Barrow Beetle, now has more HP than the foes before it (about 7,800, was 4,100) and hits harder. The zone 1 boss's Venom and the zone 2 boss's Bleed now tick for a share of your health, as their hits do (about 1.5% a tick, was under 1%). Best shot: zone4-boss.

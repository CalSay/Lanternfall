Tooling only, no change to the game. Our automatic checks run on six test machines, and each had grown to take 10 to 13 minutes, more than half the 20-minute limit, so a machine that hit a one-off failure had no time left to try again. Each machine now runs its share of the checks as two halves side by side (most checks spend their time waiting on the game's clock, so two fit at once), and the shares are re-balanced from the latest measured times. Every check still runs and asserts the same thing.

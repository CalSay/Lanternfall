Close the game mid-fight and your hero no longer sits idle: they go back to the last place they gathered and keep working while you're away. The chip under Fight / Gather says where before you go ("Leave now: Wren goes back to the Pine Grove, about 50K Pine Log in 8 hours"), and the away card says "You left mid-fight, so Wren went back to the Pine Grove." Fights still earn nothing while you're away. Best shot: away-card.

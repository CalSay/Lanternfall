Every ability now has its own effect in a fight. Arrows leave glowing trails, hits flash and spark, and big finishers shake the screen. Each status has one colour for every hero: Bleed is red, Burning orange, Chilled blue, Marked gold. When a status lands, its icon pops up by the foe and the foe glows in that colour while it lasts. Shots aim at the foe's chest. With reduced motion on, you see a still glow and the icon instead. Best shot: echo-arrow.

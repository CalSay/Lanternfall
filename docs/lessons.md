# Lessons

What the build threads learned, grouped by work area. Rules, not history: each line says what to do and why.

**Read** the sections for your card's areas before you start (a card usually touches 1 to 3).
**Add** a line after any correction (from Cal, a reviewer, CI, a blocked tool, a surprise) in the same PR as the fix.
Format: `- Rule in one imperative sentence. Why: evidence. (card, date)`
If a line is already there, sharpen it instead of adding a second. If a line turns out wrong, fix or delete it.
Lessons that did not come from a correction (predicted vs measured effect of a card) go in the Autopilot
`lessons.md` prediction log, not here.

## Combat and balance

- Treat a DECISIONS.md line the code never matched as a question, not a fact: check the code before building on it. Why: "No healing between fights" (2026-10-02) sat beside a 15% heal per kill and a full heal on a loss, so losing was the better heal until the normal-death-says-so judge ruled. (normal-death-says-so, 2026-10-08)
- Never gate "Boss ready" on the old damage estimate; use the 30-scratch-fight estimate at the player's own parry and dodge record. Why: the old estimate read 0.4 to 0.5 at the Zone 1 boss that all three starters beat, and 0.03 on a late save. (boss-readiness, 2026-10-06)
- Keep the readiness estimate running while a tip pauses the game, and include Deepwell boons, gear changes made while gathering, and the sim seed. Why: Codex found each of these in rounds 1 to 3 of PR #47. (boss-readiness, 2026-10-06)
- Check what a foe really does in turn fights before carding words for it: read the 59k riders and the 59b get-up. The legacy pack behaviours (Wraith heal, bat dive, spore cloud, golem slam) never run there, and zones 6-14 have no elites. Why: the first foe-tricks card promised "Healed", "Guarded" and "Frozen: you lose this turn", none of which a zone 6-12 foe can do. (foe-tricks-say-so, 2026-10-08)
- CI's eyes job replays a route in real time (no `--frozen`), so a moment that landed on step 17 locally may land on step 30 there, or never before the zone boss. Give a luck-timed moment a kills-0 fixture, enough steps that it always lands, and an expect on a body flag the handler sets and keeps; take the moment's shots locally with `--frozen`. Why: foe-tricks-say-so passed every view locally and failed two of them in CI. (foe-tricks-say-so, 2026-10-08)
- Give every scratch fight its own seed (turnCombatSample hashes seed and fight index; the first fight keeps the caller's seed). Why: one LCG stream across a chain correlated long fights, so one seed read 13-32% where independent fights read 53-67%. (sampler-independence, 2026-10-06)
- Run `node tools/health.mjs --compare` before and after any balance or pacing change. Why: it is the only before/after measure and CI gates on it. (f-health, 2026-10-05)
- Play the persona's whole turn skill (parry, dodge and ability rings) when measuring pacing for a named player type. The sim's own turn player lands 80% of defences and never presses a ring, so every timed ability is a Miss. Why: Codex P1 on PR #52, where one shared 40% defence rate stood in for the casual. (xp-gold-pacing-report, 2026-10-06)
- difficulty-budget: predicted the budget would find bosses near the 70% casual aim with a few outliers; measured 55 of 156 hero cells out of band (zones 5-15 and elites 100%, zones 25-34 Captains 0-1% for Wren and Pip, the Fenmother easier than her Captains). Miss. Also: `turnCombatSample` on one seed correlates long fights (13-32% vs 53-67% independent), so give every boss fight its own hashed seed when measuring win rates. Why: the judge caught it before the baseline. (difficulty-budget, 2026-10-06)

- Tune a level curve against the old game on the same seeds (3 seeds, each starter), never one run. Why: one seed moved Tobin's hours to zone 30 from 19 to 41 with no change that touched him, and the old game itself ranged 25 to 46 h for Wren. (hero-progression-rework, 2026-10-06)
- Most hero XP comes from away time. A level curve with no exponential wall needs a brake past the road, or heroes run 20+ levels ahead; brake only far past it, or levels stop at walls, where they help most. (hero-progression-rework, 2026-10-06)
- Cap any level lift for a joining hero at the level of the hero who leaves, with the road's own level as the floor. Why: the judge found a join lead could put a joiner above the hero they replace, rewarding switching for its own sake. (hero-progression-rework, 2026-10-06)
- When a build or other per-player choice must stay out of a system, list every caller of the shared power function in the neutrality test. Why: PR #58 kept away, raid and farm build-neutral, but the Deepwell's depth anchor read turnPowerNow, which carried the Might multiplier (Opus review). (hero-progression-rework, 2026-10-06)
- Measure a boss on the gear the player has when they first meet it, not the gear a kept-up hero could have: the same boss was a 100% pat on the head at rare +5 and a 15% wall at common +0. Why: boss hits scale with the reference hero's HP, which grew 1.9x a zone from zone 8 while a first-hour hero's grew 1.3-1.5x. (boss-tiers, 2026-10-07)
- A first-hour budget row takes the level, gear tier and mastery a player arrives with, not only the gear: `arrivalLv` replays the XP of `ZONE_FIGHTS` fights and the boss a zone (it gives the walk's 15-18 at zones 10-13), and a fixture save's mastery stars are capped. Check the sampler on the walk's own saved game before trusting a footing change. Why: the z13 row sat at level 21, tier 3 and the mid save's x1.35 stars, reading 63% casual where the walk lost 6-42 tries; and on Wren's own walk save the sampler reads 33-56% at zones 10-12, which she never loses. (z13-arrival-footing, 2026-10-08)
- `budget.mjs --eval` now runs before the foe spawns, so a TURN_TUNE.boss change reaches its setup (hcap, hitX and hpX are read at spawn). Why: a hitCap eval first showed no effect because the boss was already built. (boss-tiers-pr3, 2026-10-07)
- Tune boss knots per zone on both hpX and hitX, not hitX alone: zones 16 and 19 needed hitX 4+ to reach 70% casual while the fight lasted 3.6 turns; lengthening the fight (hpX) fixed it without hits that one-shot. Keep boss pay on the old length curve when hpX moves. Why: the same win rate came from very different fights. (boss-tiers-pr3, 2026-10-07)
- Set `S.maxZone` to the boss's zone in any budget row or test that meets a boss for the first time. Why: the footing floor only applies to a boss not beaten yet, and the early and mid fixture saves already had a higher `maxZone`, so the z5 and z15 kept-up rows read as replays and the floor did nothing (boss-tiers-pr5-build, 2026-10-07).
- When a first-hour row will not move with `hitX`, look at the pr4 `hpFloor` knots before anything else. Why: Pip's z13 and z14 first hour sat at 40-46 whatever `hitX` did, because the class-neutral knots bound her bigger HP pool; lowering the knots 10-20% lifted her to 58-63 and left Wren alone. (boss-tiers-pr5-build, 2026-10-07)
- To test one floor in `turnLand`, turn the others off in the same test (`hpFloor [[1, 0]]`, `hitCap [[1, 0]]`). Why: at z10 the pr4 knots sat above the footing floor for a kept-up Wren, so the new floor looked broken when it was only hidden. (boss-tiers-pr5-build, 2026-10-07)
- When `hitX` is cut far, the `hpFloor` becomes the hit and the footing floor stops biting the kept-up hero: check the never-defends player on the kept-up rows before shipping, and move `hpFloor` with `hitX`. Why: z13-unstick's first fit (hitX and hpX only) put the first-time rows in band but let a geared hero who never parries or dodges win 45-100% at zones 13-15; raising the z13-15 hpFloor kept them at 0%. (z13-unstick, 2026-10-08)
- When a first-time budget row will not climb past about 50% whatever the hit knots do, check the boss's riders: Bleed, Burn and Venom tick a share of the reference HP (`TURN_TUNE.heroDot`), which no hit knot, floor or cap touches, so one Bleed tick took a third of a first-time hero's health at zone 16. Why: z16-wall's knots-only fit stalled at 40-52% casual; with the ticks off the same knots read 97/79/97. (z16-wall, 2026-10-08)
- When you add a budget row on a new footing, check every dressing step in `tools/budget.mjs` `setup()` for that footing, not only level and gear. Why: #229's z19 arrival row (tier 1 common +0) also wore the grade-4 crafted set the kept-up rows get from zone 19, which lifted a first-time Wren about 9 points; z19-wall skips the set on the arrival footing. (z19-wall, 2026-10-08)
- On an arrival-footing fit past zone 19, check whether the fight is gate-bound (about 4.8 turns) before moving hpX: once it is, hpX barely moves the win rate, and the levers are hitX (Wren's hit leaves the hpFloor first, so it trims her alone) and a per-hero heroHitX. Why: z20-wall's z23 Pip read 57/51 at every hpX from x0.2 to x1, and hitX x1.15 to x1.3 cut Wren 10-20 points while Pip held. (z20-wall, 2026-10-08)
- When a card moves a zone's boss knots, grep tools/check.mjs for the old values (`16 * 2.8`-style want tables) and run every C29 section, not only the asserts you edited. Why: z20-wall's full check failed "boss pass: a boss lasts longer" on zone 20's old hpX after the targeted sections passed (z20-wall, 2026-10-08).
- boss-tiers-pr5b: predicted tricks, gates and a kept-up footing floor at zones 16-34 would hold casual 59-86 with the G4 set worn and keep uniques safe; measured casual mean 72-74 on all 13 rows (Wren/Pip spread 7-28, Tobin 77-83, never-defends 0-1%, casualHigh 95-100), +50% health 0-2 points with the floor and +20-35 without, damage x2 casual 88-100 with gates and 100 on every row without. Hit. Fitting by search (hitX and Tobin's heroHitX per zone, mean of Wren and Pip to 70) converged in 5 iterations, but the fit absorbs whatever the mechanics do, so test each lever by switching it off and watching the stress rows, not the fitted rows. Numbers are provisional until the crafting balance pass. (boss-tiers-pr5b, 2026-10-07)
- Any change to zone 1 trash fight length moves `health` firstGoalSec (the first boss win): run `node tools/health.mjs --compare` before pushing, not only check and budget. Why: early-foes-three-hits passed check and budget, then CI health failed at 107 s against 58 s; a 6-Attack zone 1 floor was cut to 3, and Cal accepted the rest (73 s, optimiser zone 12 at 1 h). (early-foes-three-hits, 2026-10-07)
- Check a boss-gain cap against every pair of rules one hero can wear together and against abilities that read the changed count (Oath x Mountain reached x1.82; Crimson's 10 stacks fed Final Echo +62%), and never let a one-action boost ride a stored Bleed that `Math.max` keeps all fight. Why: the code review of uniques-first-four found all three after each rule passed alone. (uniques-first-four, 2026-10-07)

- Sample a "with this piece" fight change with at least 80 scratch fights a side on the same seeds, and show no line when the shares move less than half a step or the rounding points against the raw numbers; swap the piece into both `gsCache` and `S.equip[pos]` for the sample (59-combat reads the helm from `S.equip`). Why: 40 a side read a tier 4 Warblade into an empty slot as "7 in 10, not 8 in 10", and a gsCache-only swap left the new helm's armour out (review, craft-delta, 2026-10-08)
- When a fight rule reads its io (a gate skip, a heal, a foe's HP), assert it on the live path (`turnCombatTick` with `TURN_LIVE_IO`) as well as through `turnCombatSample`, which builds its own io. Why: the live fight built each fight before it set the new foe, so the rally-gate skip read the last foe's 0 HP and every live boss from #160 to 8 Oct skipped its gates, while the budget and every boss fit had them on. (rally-gates-live, 2026-10-08)

## Economy and skilling

- Ask Cal before `node tools/health.mjs --write-baseline`, naming each moved metric, which way it moved and the judge's ruling. Why: rewriting the bar CI scores against was blocked as a CI bypass until Cal said yes (gold-without-training, 2026-10-07).
- Read `docs/design/systems-map.md` flags before touching an economy card. Open flags: wood and essence pile up, iron ore is idle (Transmute is lossy), cobalt needs Mining 64 with no hint, Smithing is dead past 54, gold is the only mid-game choke, Renown/Stamps/boss tokens have no spend, tents 6 to 10 are unbuildable, the Renown Day omen does nothing. (systems-map, 2026-10-06)
- Register any new currency-like counter in the systems map with a source and a sink. Why: the check fails on a currency with no source or sink, and on an unregistered counter. (systems-map, 2026-10-06)
- Check any reward or shop design against the standing reward lines in `DECISIONS.md` (enemies never drop crafting materials; gold is the flat camp budget; the Armoury owns bag room and loadouts) before proposing what it pays. Why: the monetisation red team found caches paying materials and a paid Armoury room that the Armoury building already sells. (monetisation plan, 2026-10-06)
- Read and pay an Essence cost with `matOwn`/`matPay`/`essPay`, never `S.mats.ess[t - 1]`, and name it with `costName`. Why: Essence is one pile across grades, so a grade-indexed read shows "not enough" while the total covers it. (counters-and-layers, 2026-10-07)
- Give a budget one limit, not two. Why: star points never bound while a 2-lit cap held cost to 6 and income passed 6 by Lv 18; the cap went and the points became the only limit. (counters-and-layers, 2026-10-07)
- Price a first-hour build under the gold every hero is sure to hold when its plot opens (360 when zone 1 clears, the same for all three starters), and give any gold on a guide step a `['gold', 0, n]` row so the step says "Win N more gold" and never pauses. Why: before the Workbench's price, no gold was spent in the first hour (8,138 unspent at 60 min), and a gold cost the guide cannot see would hold a paused step it cannot finish; browser checks that stage the Workbench with logs only also need `S.gold` set. (workbench-cost, 2026-10-07)
- When a change puts a new step between gathering and spending (refining), run `node tools/health.mjs --compare` before opening the PR and teach the sim player the step everywhere it spends (crafts, upgrades and the re-roll loop), not only where it crafts. Why: CI's health gate failed refine-queues on Essence held (the re-roll loop starved of middles) and a Tobin dry stretch (a sim hearth trip skipped the gather window). (refine-queues, 2026-10-08)
- Before fitting a station curve on `sim.mjs`, check where its station XP comes from (`--report skills --parts C` splits refining from the rest): the mixed policy's re-roll loop was 97% of it, so a curve fitted on that player is fitted to grinding the rarity die. Part C plays the kept-up player (`--reroll 0`, stations left refining All while away). (craft-curve-skills-report, 2026-10-09)
- A rule keyed on "the station's top tier" must read the station the XP goes to, not the item's: reforge pays Enchanting, so its below-top-tier share reads `skillTopTier('ench')`, not the item's own station (reviewer catch). (craft-curve-skills-report, 2026-10-09)
- When a switch rewrites what a perk says, switch its old effect off with it (gate the modifier or the Omen's `when` on the switch), and grep every reader of the old key: the words alone change nothing. Why: with `CRAFT_TUNE.grades` on, Forge 5 and Steady Hands read "Crafts grade as N levels higher" while their `rareW` odds boost still lifted the tool die (code reviewer). (craft-attribute-grades, 2026-10-09)
- A budget fixture built from a by-zone level table must also respect the gates that table implies: wear the best tier the station has open, and apply a row's own tier shift before reading the grade. Why: the first graded run put tier 4 pieces on a Smithing 32 Tobin and graded the behind rows at the zone's tier, moving the gated z25-boss-behind row for the wrong reason (combat reviewer). (craft-attribute-grades, 2026-10-09)
- When a card gives a word a meaning that an earlier check's text scan bans ("grade" after #267's rename to "tier"), widen that scan's exemption to the new meaning in the same PR and keep it catching the old one. Why: the full check failed on the Fine Work and grade-line strings until craft-shortfall-offer's scan allowed "grade" before a letter (check.mjs). (craft-attribute-grades, 2026-10-09)
- A check that crafts more than once and then reads a grade must reset the station's level and XP first: every craft trains the station, so the grade climbs under the check. Why: the Strike checks' "Infuse short of Essence" case read grade A (no Infuse to refuse) after a dozen Pine Bows had raised Woodcraft from 7 (check.mjs). (craft-strike-infuse, 2026-10-09)
- When a card adds a field to a registered state, grep check.mjs for asserts that compare that state to a literal (`JSON.stringify(S.craft) === ...`) and update them in the same PR. Why: the old "crafting" section's defaults literal failed on every fixture once `made` joined S.craft (code reviewer). (craft-strike-infuse, 2026-10-09)
- A full-screen press target must stay up a beat after it resolves, ignoring presses, so a second tap cannot land on the button under it. Why: a double tap in the Strike's gold could hit the row's Craft or Infuse again and spend a second set of materials (code reviewer). (craft-strike-infuse, 2026-10-09)
- A timing press the player makes in a menu needs its own clock and a game hold, and must pay only when it resolves; a hidden tab resolves it as a miss. Why: the fight's bar is private to the turn UI and only draws in a wind-up, and a craft paid before the press would lose materials to a closed tab (craft-strike-infuse spec). (craft-strike-infuse, 2026-10-09)
- No hard progress walls. Why: walls are the top long-play quit reason in the research set (99 mentions); the 50h run shows 9 to 10 stalls of an hour or more per hero and a 12h wall near zones 24 to 25. (f-fun-library, f-health-long)

## UI and menus
- Make a tile's look follow the phase the engine checks, not only what the move costs: the fight tiles read "Ready" in gold through the turn banner while every press was refused there, and the only answer was a shake that reduced motion turns off. Why: the desktop playtest (F11) lost presses for 5.8 s of an 8.8 s fight. How: drive the look from the same phase gate (`turnBarInfo`), and answer a refused press with something that is not an animation.
- Offer a pick only when more than one answer is sensible: check the spec's test ("A choice", `docs/design/skilling-crafting-overhaul.md:131`) before carding one. - A pacing rule that counts moments must count only moments a stall cannot make: level cards and hero lines fire during grinds and losses, so they hide the stall the rule is for. And never put a "person-minute" in a rule until a human run measures it; model players run on stepped clocks. Why: the red team on f3-restate (2026-10-08).
- A design-doc PR (decisions or a spec) runs the `docs/review/design-doc.md` hard checks before opening: coverage-map area, a numeric prediction with its measure and miss threshold, how to switch it off, and a check of each new rule against standing lines in `DECISIONS.md`. Why: Codex P1s on PR #55 (no area, no prediction, no rollback, bench XP clashed with the hero fatigue rule). (hero-progression review, 2026-10-06)
- A beat map is a contract: before opening it, check each rule it states (one new thing per 3 minutes, big-moment gaps) against every row, for every hero pick. Why: Codex found four rule breaks across PRs #73 to #83 that a row-by-row pass would have caught. (early-game lead, 2026-10-06)
- Every decision line that adds behaviour names its own switch-off flag and what the game does with it off, and a beat that adds a hero join counts the join as a new thing for every pick. Why: Codex P1s on PR #86 (no Champion-card rollback; a Tobin pick met Switching and the Codex in one beat). (early-game lead, 2026-10-06)
- Before tuning a system (a curve, a price, a cap), say what it is for and check it still does that job; if not, it becomes a design card.

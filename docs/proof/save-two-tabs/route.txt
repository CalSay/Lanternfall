# seed: 1
# Proof for card save-two-tabs: with the game open in two tabs, the older tab stops saving and says so; the newer progress
# survives. Before the fix the older tab wrote its stale save over the newer one (repro: the next load read about 1 gold, not 12,345).
# Part 1, one tab: the card never shows.
new early
wait 3
expect "Attack"
wait 12
expect-no "open in another tab"
# Part 2, coming back to the older tab: tab 2 opens the same game (tab 1 goes to the background) with 12,345 gold, plays and saves.
tab new gold=12345
wait 8
expect "Attack"
expect-no "open in another tab"
expect-save gold>=12345
tab 1
expect "Lanternfall is open in another tab. Reload to keep playing here."
expect "Reload"
shot older-tab
wait 8
expect-save gold>=12345
# Reload in the older tab: it now plays the newer save, with no card.
tap "Reload"
wait 3
expect-no "open in another tab"
expect-save gold>=12345
# Part 3, closing the older tab without coming back to it (its pagehide save): tab 3 opens with 54,321 gold and saves;
# tab 1 is closed from the background.
tab new gold=54321
wait 8
expect-save gold>=54321
tab close 1
expect-save gold>=54321
# Tab 2 (also older) comes back to the front: it shows the card and does not save.
tab 2
expect "open in another tab"
wait 8
expect-save gold>=54321
# A fresh load reads the newest progress, with no card.
tab new
wait 3
expect-no "open in another tab"
expect-save gold>=54321
shot fresh-load

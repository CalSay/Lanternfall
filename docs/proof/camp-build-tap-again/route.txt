# seed: 1
# Proof for card camp-build-tap-again. Run: grep -v '^#' route.txt | node tools/playtest.mjs batch --seed 1 (then --landscape).
# `new` empties the shots folder, so the shots here were taken one part at a time and named -portrait / -landscape.
# The -1280x720 shots are the same route in a 1280x720 mouse window (browser-first; playtest.mjs has no desktop flag yet).
# Part 1: the fire is lit, 12 Pine Log and 300 gold in hand, Hesketh at "Build a Workbench".
# One tap on the Workbench card's Build button starts the build (no "Tap again").
new bench-ready
wait 2
tap-if "Continue"
tap "Camp"
wait 1
expect "Build a Workbench"
shot guide-points-at-bench
tap "Build Lv 1, 10s"
expect "Building Workbench Lv 1"
expect-save gold=0
shot bench-one-tap

# Part 2: the Watchtower is not a guide step (zone 4 Dipper save, 2700 gold, Watchtower affordable). The first tap arms
# "Tap again" and builds nothing; a second tap 4 s later (past the old 4 s window) still builds.
new dipper-z4
wait 2
tap-if "Continue"
tap "Camp"
wait 1
tap "Build Lv 1, 45s"
expect "Tap again"
expect-no "Building Watchtower"
shot armed-tap-again
wait 4
expect "Tap again"
tap "Tap again to build Lv 1"
expect "Building Watchtower Lv 1"
shot built-after-4s

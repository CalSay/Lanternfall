# seed: 1
# Proof for card deepwell-spillover-keeps-fight. A Deepwell run that was started (or resumed) while gathering keeps its fight when
# the tab sits in the background and Spillover moves the hero to the next node.
# The save (tests/proof-fixtures/save-deep-spill.json, made by loading and saving save-late through the game): Pip at zone 38,
# cutting at the Tideash Grove with its pile 3,000 short of full and Spillover on, and a paused Deepwell run on floor 1 that was
# started from there.
#   resumed-*.png   the run resumed from the Switch activity picker: Pip's turn against the Deep Spore Cap
#   away-*.png      an hour in the background: the card shows Tideash and, after the pile filled, Mangrove from Spillover
#   back-*.png      back in the Deepwell: the same Deep Spore Cap at full health, Pip's turn, the Act / Skills / Foe dock live
# Before the fix, the away hour's Spillover reset the scene: the foe was left standing at 0 health, every fight button went grey,
# the Foe tab went away and the run never moved again.
# tools/check.mjs ("deepwell-spillover-keeps-fight") holds the same for Wren, Tobin and Pip and at 1280x720, 740x360 and 360x740.
new deep-spill
wait 3
tap-if "Dismiss this tip"
tap "Switch activity"
wait 1
tap "Deepwell · Floor 1"
wait 4
tap-if "Got it"
expect "Your boons this run"
expect ".sb-tab[data-tab=foe]"
shot resumed
background 1
expect "You kept working the Tideash Grove."
expect "Mangrove"
shot away
tap "Collect"
wait 3
expect "Your boons this run"
expect ".sb-tab[data-tab=foe]"
shot back

# Proof for card ux-l1-gather-tip-after-loss (tooling only). No player-visible change: nothing in src/ changed, so there is nothing
# to play. The guide was not at fault: a player's game runs on, the guide looks every 250 ms, and the Gather tip shows in the gap
# after the next kill (about half a second of play) and holds the game there.
# Cause: the UX-L1 walk drives the clock itself. With no step to press it ran 20 ticks of 0.1 s in one go and looked again only after
# them. A turn fight takes about 2 s, so the 2 s jump could fall in step with the fights and end mid-fight every time, never in the
# gap. A lost boss fight (Try again card, the boss held back, the fights restarting) shifted the fights into that step. A probe
# per tick after a forced loss read the gap (Gather step returned) at ticks 3 to 6 of every jump while every jump ended on the
# hero's turn, for 150+ passes while the zone climbed 4 to 8 (CI's dump: zone 9, step null after 293 idle passes).
# The tip did not retire: GUIDE_RT.hid and GUIDE_RT.shown.gather stayed empty in every probe. "step null" in the dump is what
# onboardStep() returns mid-fight for a started between step (it waits for the gap), so a mid-fight read looked like a dead end.
# Fix (tools/check.mjs, UX-L1 walk only): the idle jump stops at the first tick onboardStep() returns a step. The walk also loses to
# the zone boss every run as the Gather tip comes due (before pressing it; the loss is counted by S.bossTry.tries, since Auto may try
# the boss again at once), and asserts the tip is back on screen (ringed and pressable) within 120 s of play.
# The new assert on the OLD walk (4 runs at once, 740x360 and 844x390): back after never and 326 s (failed, one with "step null" as on
# CI), and 89, 85, 45, 22, 10 s on the others. An earlier version (timing the step, not the tip on screen) failed 4 of 8 the same way.
# The new walk: node tools/check.mjs --only="UX-L1" passed at 740x360, 844x390, 1280x720 and 1920x1080 (back after 11, 13, 27, 9 s).
# Loops (sizes 740x360, 844x390, 1280x720; 4 at once beside 2 at a 6x CPU throttle), final code: the guide walk passed 90 of 90
# views, 30 of 30 under the throttle, tip back after 7 to 36 s. One run failed a different UX-L1 assert outside this card (the
# mid-game layout part, 740x360: the Attack sheet with Training "not open"), in a fresh page this change does not touch; reported
# to the coordinator as a find, not fixed here (the card puts other UX-L1 asserts out of scope).

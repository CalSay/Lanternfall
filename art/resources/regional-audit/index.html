Grades4–6. Salt, tides, storm cliffs and drowned ruins. Flexible marine materials progress toward armoured mythical predators.

Grades7–9. Ash, buried fire and fallen light. Heat-adapted sources progress toward draconic resilience.

Grades10–12. Bitter mountain cold and fallen starlight. Tough physical materials give way to star-touched structures.

Grades13–15. Lifeless grey woodland and black water. Preserved remains and spectral manifestations, not a flourishing ecosystem.

Sources, visuals and progression

How the encounters must look

Ground and marsh

Keep feet and spear tips registered to the creature's silhouette. Make attack tells, contact, hurt and defeat readable for each anatomy. A larger beast must not become a recoloured small one.

Water and shoreline

A shark stays in the water. The hero braces on a rock shelf; a visible waterline covers the submerged body. A wake precedes the jaw surge into spear range. Author surfacing, splash, hurt and submerging assets; recover the resource through the gathering result.

Flight and spectral life

Flying beasts need perched/landing or diving contact windows. Spectral sources condense into tangible remains through lantern light; show authored transformation frames. No arbitrary floating pelt or invisible strike.

RECOMMENDED SUPPORTING-RESOURCE CORRECTIONS These are complete design choices for review, not new implemented drops or currencies. All existing ore names and indices remain unchanged. SECONDARY MATERIALS — one fuel, tanning salt and dye per region Hollow: none. Keep early recipes raw and simple. Coast: Sea Coal — exposed fossil seams in sea cliffs; black nodules with pale shell inclusions. Brinesalt — scrape mineral crust from exposed salt pans; broad white crystals. Murex Dye — pigment deposits in abandoned reef-snail shells; purple material in a ribbed shell. No new tide lockout. Emberwaste: Cinder Coal — ancient buried coal reheated beneath the Kilns; fractured dark chunks with rust-red seams. Glass Salt — evaporite crust caught between cooled glass ridges; pale angular crust, not edible glass or a gem. Cinder Ochre — collect mineral pigment from ash-clay banks; dull red powder cake. This avoids a fresh green dye crop before the region is healed. Pale Reach: Frost Peat — cut preserved old bog beds exposed beneath snow; fibrous dark slabs, not a living green bog. Rime Salt — scrape salt-rich mineral crust at Frostgate; short white-blue needles distinct from ordinary ice. Frostcap Dye — pale blue pigment from sheltered cold-adapted fungi under old Rimewood roots; squat blue caps, no warm red/green foliage. Gloamvale: Dead Coal — dense coal exposed beneath ancient ruined foundations; flat near-black blocks. Grey Salt — mineral crust at old Blind Mere margins; low grey shelves. Inkcap Dye — preserved powder from dead inkcap remains in Coldhearth; collapsed grey-violet caps, no living fungal garden. Why these are stronger: fuels tolerate the region's forging demands; salts prepare its tougher hides; dyes bind its more unusual fibres. Their region identity is not a hidden damage bonus. These twelve designs replace both conflicting secondary-name tables; old alternatives should be retired together in materials.md and gear-2.md after approval. RAW MATERIALS AND REFINING Wood is gathered as logs, fibre as raw strands, hides as skins/pelts, herbs as plant material and Gems as intact stones or rough mineral pieces. Do not display a field of finished linen or a harvested roll of cloth. Region 2 onward: ore -> ingot; wood -> plank; hide -> leather; fibre -> cloth; herb -> tincture. Keep existing recipe role and grade. Use a material stem for the finished name: Tide Kelpie Hide -> Tide Kelpie Leather, Duskfang Pelt -> Duskfang Leather, Hemp Fibre -> Hemp Cloth. No 'Hide Leather' or 'Leather Leathers'. Sea Silk is raw byssus fibre; Sea Silk Cloth is the woven product. The loot icon and refined icon need distinct silhouettes. Gems remain family key crystal but player-facing label Gems. Replace the blanket 'Shard' suffix with 'Gem' as the generic family unit, or supply full per-grade display names: Tide Pearl is an intact pearl, never 'Pearl Shard'. A mineable uplifted shellstone pocket avoids inventing a new underwater harvesting control. No Gem Ingot or Pearl Ingot. SIGILS — keep the four established families Coast: Tide Sigil (internal family pearl). Emberwaste: Cinder Sigil (glass). Pale Reach: Frost Sigil (star). Gloamvale: Gloam Sigil (well). They are socket items, not crafting materials. Give them carved/inscribed shapes and approved rarity treatment; resource gems have no Sigil border or aura. Individual names remain '<boss or theme> Sigil'. A Coast buff/currency reference that still says Pearl must become Tide Sigil; only the crafting gem uses Tide Pearl. No new Sigil family, socket mechanic or yield is proposed. CURRENCIES — retain their existing roles Gold: coin stack, warm yellow; ordinary economy. Renown: laurel medal; live bounty and hero-unlock currency. Embers: cooled coal-like shards with orange seams; raid/relic currency. Distinguish the currency from Pip's class meter through its label and UI context. Do not add regional currencies merely because a region has new materials. PROGRESSION AND IMPLEMENTATION CORRECTIONS Planned grades: Hollow 1-3, Coast 4-6, Emberwaste 7-9, Pale Reach 10-12, Gloamvale 13-15. Current code differs: only five material grades exist; Essence zone thresholds are 1/7/13/19/42, and gathering unlocks use skill levels 1/14/30/64/112 without region checks. Therefore the first five icons cannot be treated as one icon per region, and changing art does not fix access. Recommended access rule for coordinator design: a grade requires BOTH its region to be reached AND its skill/progression requirement; previously unlocked grades remain accessible. Do not set unmeasured new numeric thresholds in this art review. Fight-drop grade must use the same regional grade band as gathering, with a separately approved within-region progression rule. Grades retain their intended power values. Implementation must reconcile name/source tables, recipes/refined-name formatting, node labels, colours, item names, Bestiary/gathering text, preview metadata and onboarding examples. Expanding to fifteen grades also touches state/inventory lengths, live and away drop calculation, UI loops, save handling and tests. Coordinator controls that gameplay scope; this audit changes no live balances, arrays or saves. ARTWORK CONSEQUENCES The current 45-icon preview is a draft subset. This design calls for all 105 primary slots (including the 15 unchanged ores), plus twelve secondary icons, four Sigil family treatments, three currency icons and the current/future trophy sets. Existing first-five Ore art stays. Revise only accepted name/source changes after this full design is reviewed. Production rules

  • Stable material-family keys and grade indices stay intact. Naming, source placement and region unlocks are separate changes.
  • Raw plants, threads, hides and wood must look harvestable. Finished cloth, planks, leather, ingots and tinctures get separate station outputs. Strip redundant nouns from refined item names: Duskfang Pelt becomes Duskfang Leather.
  • The source property explains the fantasy; existing grade power remains the numerical authority. These descriptions do not add resistances, abilities or new combat mechanics. Low-tide scenes, spit tells, wingbeats and spectral transformation describe authored poses only; they do not introduce tide lockouts, swimming controls or extra enemy attacks.
  • All packs need complete states and artist-made effects. Review icons at16px,22px and larger inspection scale; retain distinct silhouettes without relying solely on colour.
  • The current five-grade live gates differ from the future three-grades-per-region specification. The coordinator must approve and test regional access separately; no silent save remap or balance change.

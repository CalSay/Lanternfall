Scope: proposal artifact only; no game, art, save, economy, or source edits. User direction: all five regions and all 15 grades; fantasy-forward; do not sideline later regions; keep ores unchanged. Grade rows and approved gem names are sourced from docs/design/materials.md; region contexts from docs/design/lore.md, region-2.md and regions-4-5.md; runtime evidence from 20-data.js, 21-data-craft.js, 40-rules.js, 50-sim.js and 55-gathering.js.

Files

- Grades 1–5 are live names. Crystal grades 6–15 are owner-approved MAT1 display names in docs, but are not in live MAT arrays. Essence grades 6–15 are draft names from materials.md section 3 and need owner approval.

Name and source consistency
- The live gem key stays crystal, the family label is Gems, and the raw stack name is <gem> Shard. Therefore grade-4 raw material is Pearl Shard.
- Pearl at grade 4 is a raw gem. Tide Sigil is the Coast buff-item family/category; the old buff family id pearl is not a gem stack. Individual Sigils use <boss/theme> Sigil names. Never label a Tide Sigil as Pearl or count it in crystal stock.
- There is stale, conflicting language in docs/design/region-2.md: sections 2, 3, 5, 6 and 9 still use Pearls/Lantern Pearls for Tide-Pool finds, settings, drops, costs, bounties and currency. MAT1 materials.md section 7 and gear-2.md MAT1 note say Coast buff/currency uses become Tide Sigils. Only Pearl Shard should mean the gem. Reconcile this Coast doc before implementation.
- Generic crystal source is Mining geodes; MAT1 says gems are mined from Geodes and CRAFT_FAMILY says Quarry Golems drop a few. Make the Coast Pearl deposit reef/shellstone so its organic name has a grounded habitat. Pale Reach Starscar gathers Frost Sigils, not gems; Gloamvale Blind Mere/Stillwood gather Gloam Sigils, not gems. Do not reuse those Sigil activities as gem nodes without a separate node spec.
- The MAT1 refined-good rule appends the refined unit to the exact raw name (e.g. Hide + Leather, Ore + Ingot). Gems are not one of the five refined families, so the raw form is Pearl Shard; do not invent Pearl Ingot. Essence keeps the unit Essence: Lucent Essence, etc.
- Gem rank is a regional/economic grade, not a monotonic hardness claim. Pearl, Opal, Sunstone, Moonstone and Onyx do not form a strict physical hardness progression. No code should infer combat strength from mineral hardness. JSON strength states material grade only.
- Essence grades 6–15 proposal has four owner-review points: Lucent after Starlit may read softer; Dawnlit may imply daylight before Emberwaste healing; Undimmed/Unfading are close synonyms; Old Light may sound aged rather than strongest. Keep the Gloamvale essence as persistent potent light against darkness, never corrupted/dark essence.

Current implementation boundary and unlock mismatch
- Runtime has only five crystal and five Essence names/colors. 21-data-craft.js defines crystal/Essence arrays and five crystal NODE_NAMES; 20-data.js defines shared Essence MAT and matName.
- Current Essence grade assignment is zone-based in 40-rules.js: PACE.essTier = [1,7,13,19,42]. Zone 19 is tier 4 inside Hollow/Region 1 (zones 1–35); tier 5 begins at zone 42 in Coast/Region 2 (zones 36–70). This does not match the intended grade/region map: 1–3 Hollow, 4–6 Coast, 7–9 Emberwaste, 10–12 Pale Reach, 13–15 Gloamvale.
- Gathered node tiers are skill-based: NODE_REQ [1,14,30,64,112], independent of region. Tier 4 can open by skill before Coast; tier 5 also has no Region 2 gate. gear-2.md section 0 explicitly says current tiers follow zones and skill, not regions. Do not imply the future grade-region gates are implemented.
- Grade expansion is broader than renaming: separately specify one region-to-grade rule for fight drops and gathered nodes, then update data arrays, state inventory lengths, UI/codex max counts, colors/icons, away simulation and tests together. This is gameplay/data scope and was not changed here.

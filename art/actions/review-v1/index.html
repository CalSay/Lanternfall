Actions of the Lantern Road

Six hero moves, two shared actions and eight interface states. Shared controls use charcoal, steel, ivory and worn brass. Violet echoes for Wren, worn steel and brass for Tobin, living embers for Pip.

Design review — artwork is not wired into the game. Timer numbers and labels stay accessible UI text. No new abilities or mechanics are implied.

In the action bar

Native display samples: 16, 24 and 64 px. Source artwork is larger than the final UI grid; final palette and pixel-grid treatment remains a separate production pass. Try both sampling modes. The eight state assets are a shared vocabulary for future ability slots.
